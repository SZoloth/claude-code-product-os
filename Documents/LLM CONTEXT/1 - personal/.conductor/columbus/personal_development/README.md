# Personal Development

This directory contains all personal development content, organized in a MECE (Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive) structure that consolidates what was previously split between "Therapy and personal growth" and "Strategic Life Planning/Personal Development".

## Directory Structure

### Core Plans & Philosophy/
**Purpose**: Strategic development planning and foundational philosophy
- Master development plans and systematic approaches
- Personal philosophy and operating systems
- Long-term strategic thinking and life design

### Skills & Frameworks/  
**Purpose**: Structured methodologies and therapeutic approaches
- RODBT (Radically Open Dialectical Behavior Therapy) skills
- Systematic development frameworks and coaching systems
- Evidence-based therapeutic and growth methodologies

### Resources & Guides/
**Purpose**: Reference materials and how-to content
- Practical guides on agency, social skills, conversation, and productivity
- Articles and resources from thought leaders
- Visual aids and reference materials

### Lifestyle & Activities/
**Purpose**: Life enrichment and experience planning
- Travel and activity bucket lists
- Relationship and date planning
- Recreational and experiential goals

## Navigation

Start with **Core Plans & Philosophy** for your primary development strategies, then use **Skills & Frameworks** for systematic approaches to specific areas of growth. Reference **Resources & Guides** for tactical implementation, and **Lifestyle & Activities** for enriching experiences that support overall development.

## Integration

This structure supports the Security-Agency-Connection flywheel outlined in the personal development plan, where:
- Security enables taking action despite fear
- Agency leads to solving problems and creating value
- Value creation builds stronger relationships
- Stronger relationships provide positive feedback that reinforces security
