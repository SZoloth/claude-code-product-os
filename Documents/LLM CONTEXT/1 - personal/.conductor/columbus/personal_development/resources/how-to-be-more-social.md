
How to automatically be more social:


the System is going to involve:
1)finding the events page of various Local Spots
2)subscribing to newsletters, if available
3)subscribing to calendars, if available
4)following twitter and ig pages + turning on notifications
5)anything that doesn’t offer 2-4 add to a spreadsheet and check their events page every sunday
6)sign up as the events get posts / every sunday
7)share events you’re going to with local friends and encourage them to invite their own friends

i created a group chat with my local friends where i tell people what events i’m going to. they’re free to join me but i’ll be going regardless

to make this more specific, first figure out the genre of events you want to go to. for me they were:
-live music
-socials
-cultural events
-academic talks

“How do I find the Local Spots?”

Google + Google Maps the following keywords:

live music:
-club
-jazz bar
-concert hall
-opera house
-instagram pages

don’t sleep on clubs, they often put on actual concerts

also don’t sleep on opera houses. i’m gonna see a Sultan’s of Swing cover here


instagram
search for “live music <city>, like a bunch of posts and Reels then the algorithm will slowly push you local curation pages that you can check every sunday or turn on notifications for

socials:
-coworking
-coliving
-social clubs
-MeetUp

the first two no body talks about. these places often have a Social Club built in which hosts public events

cultural events:
-library
-museum
-newspapers (they often have their own newsletters for events)

academics:
-every local university hosts colloquia, talks, conferences and they’re often open to the public
