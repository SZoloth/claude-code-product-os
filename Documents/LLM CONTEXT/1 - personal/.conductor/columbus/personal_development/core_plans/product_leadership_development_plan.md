# Product Leadership Development Plan
*Comprehensive 6-Month Program for Trust, Buy-in, Consensus, and Influence*

## Executive Summary

This plan is designed to transform your analytical and research strengths into differentiated product leadership capabilities. As AI tools commoditize documentation and knowledge work, your competitive advantage will come from mastering human-centered leadership skills that drive alignment across complex stakeholder environments.

**Target Outcome**: Become the product leader who can navigate 13+ department stakeholders at DreamWorks Animation with confidence, building trust, securing buy-in, and driving consensus on complex technical initiatives.

---

## Current State Assessment

### Your Strategic Advantages
- **Exceptional Research & Analysis Skills**: Proven ability to understand complex problems systematically
- **Multi-Industry Experience**: Cross-functional perspective from consulting across diverse sectors
- **Documentation & Knowledge Management**: Systems thinking approach to organizing information
- **AI/Automation Proficiency**: Technical depth that builds credibility with engineering teams
- **Strategic Planning Excellence**: Demonstrated through marathon training and comprehensive personal systems

### Leadership Development Opportunity
While you excel at the analytical foundation of product management, the next level requires mastering the human dynamics of influence, trust-building, and consensus-driving across diverse stakeholder groups.

## Four Core Leadership Skill Areas

### 1. Winning Trust
**Definition**: Building credibility and psychological safety that makes stakeholders want to follow your recommendations

### 2. Getting Buy-in  
**Definition**: Converting stakeholder understanding into active support and resource commitment

### 3. Driving Consensus/Alignment
**Definition**: Facilitating agreement across diverse perspectives and competing priorities

### 4. Influence Without Authority
**Definition**: Persuading and motivating stakeholders who don't report to you directly

---

## Skill Assessment Framework

### Self-Evaluation Rubric (Rate 1-5 for each skill)

#### Winning Trust
- **Credibility**: Do stakeholders believe you understand their domain/challenges?
- **Reliability**: Do you consistently deliver on commitments?
- **Transparency**: Do you communicate uncertainties and trade-offs openly?
- **Empathy**: Do stakeholders feel heard and understood by you?
- **Competence**: Do others view you as technically and strategically sound?

#### Getting Buy-in
- **Vision Communication**: Can you paint compelling future states?
- **Stakeholder Motivation**: Do you understand what drives each stakeholder?
- **Risk Mitigation**: Can you address concerns before they become objections?
- **Resource Justification**: Can you make clear business cases for initiatives?
- **Urgency Creation**: Can you help stakeholders prioritize your initiatives?

#### Driving Consensus/Alignment
- **Facilitation**: Can you run productive meetings with diverse stakeholders?
- **Conflict Resolution**: Can you navigate disagreements constructively?
- **Decision Frameworks**: Do you have clear processes for group decision-making?
- **Communication Coordination**: Can you ensure all stakeholders stay informed?
- **Compromise Navigation**: Can you find win-win solutions across competing needs?

#### Influence Without Authority
- **Persuasion**: Can you change minds through logic and emotion?
- **Relationship Building**: Do you invest in long-term stakeholder relationships?
- **Coalition Building**: Can you get stakeholders to advocate for your initiatives?
- **Negotiation**: Can you find mutually beneficial outcomes?
- **Social Capital**: Do people come to you for advice and consultation?

### Initial Assessment Process
1. **Week 1**: Complete self-evaluation across all 20 dimensions
2. **Week 2**: Gather 360-degree feedback from 3-5 DreamWorks stakeholders
3. **Week 3**: Identify top 3 development priorities based on gaps and impact

## Practice Exercises

### Weekly Practice Structure
Each week includes:
1. **Theory Application** (1 hour): Study and internalize key concepts
2. **Real-World Practice** (3 hours): Apply skills in actual DreamWorks interactions
3. **Reflection & Adjustment** (30 minutes): Document learnings and adjust approach

### Trust-Building Exercises

#### Exercise 1: Stakeholder Expertise Mapping
- **Objective**: Build credibility through demonstrated understanding
- **Process**: For each of the 13 DWA departments, create detailed profiles showing:
  - Key challenges and pain points
  - Success metrics and priorities  
  - Technical requirements and constraints
  - Stakeholder personalities and communication preferences
- **Application**: Reference this knowledge in conversations to show depth of understanding
- **Measurement**: Track how often stakeholders say "you really get our department"

#### Exercise 2: Commitment Tracking System
- **Objective**: Build reliability through perfect follow-through
- **Process**: Create a system to track every commitment made to stakeholders
- **Application**: Never miss a deadline or commitment to any stakeholder
- **Measurement**: Ask stakeholders to rate your reliability monthly

#### Exercise 3: Transparent Decision Documentation
- **Objective**: Build trust through openness about decision-making
- **Process**: Document and share your decision-making process for all major recommendations
- **Application**: Show stakeholders the data, assumptions, and trade-offs behind decisions
- **Measurement**: Track stakeholder engagement with your decision documents

### Buy-in Building Exercises

#### Exercise 4: Department-Specific Value Proposition Development
- **Objective**: Tailor benefits to each stakeholder's priorities
- **Process**: For each department, create specific value propositions showing how the digital asset management system addresses their unique needs
- **Application**: Present customized benefits in all stakeholder interactions
- **Measurement**: Track commitment levels from each department

#### Exercise 5: Objection Pre-emption Strategy
- **Objective**: Address concerns before they become resistance
- **Process**: Identify potential objections from each stakeholder and prepare responses
- **Application**: Proactively address concerns in presentations and discussions
- **Measurement**: Track how often you successfully prevent objections from arising

#### Exercise 6: Success Story Collection
- **Objective**: Build compelling cases through social proof
- **Process**: Document and share wins and positive outcomes from early implementations
- **Application**: Use success stories to demonstrate value and build momentum
- **Measurement**: Track how success stories influence stakeholder enthusiasm

### Consensus-Building Exercises

#### Exercise 7: Structured Decision-Making Sessions
- **Objective**: Facilitate productive group decision-making
- **Process**: Design and run structured sessions using frameworks like RACI or Six Thinking Hats
- **Application**: Use for all major decisions involving multiple departments
- **Measurement**: Track time to decision and stakeholder satisfaction with process

#### Exercise 8: Conflict Resolution Practice
- **Objective**: Navigate disagreements constructively
- **Process**: When conflicts arise between departments, facilitate resolution sessions
- **Application**: Position yourself as the neutral facilitator who helps find solutions
- **Measurement**: Track successful conflict resolutions and relationship maintenance

#### Exercise 9: Alignment Communication Systems
- **Objective**: Keep all stakeholders informed and aligned
- **Process**: Create regular communication rhythms (weekly updates, monthly reviews)
- **Application**: Ensure no stakeholder is surprised by decisions or changes
- **Measurement**: Track stakeholder satisfaction with communication frequency and quality

### Influence Development Exercises

#### Exercise 10: Stakeholder Currency Research
- **Objective**: Understand what each stakeholder values most
- **Process**: Research each stakeholder's priorities, career goals, and success metrics
- **Application**: Frame all requests in terms of how they benefit the stakeholder
- **Measurement**: Track request approval rates by stakeholder

#### Exercise 11: Coalition Building Strategy
- **Objective**: Get stakeholders to advocate for your initiatives
- **Process**: Identify natural allies and help them see mutual benefits
- **Application**: Build support networks before making major requests
- **Measurement**: Track how often stakeholders advocate for your initiatives to others

#### Exercise 12: Value Creation Initiative
- **Objective**: Build influence through helping others succeed
- **Process**: Identify ways to help each stakeholder achieve their goals beyond the main project
- **Application**: Regularly offer assistance and resources to stakeholders
- **Measurement**: Track reciprocal support received from stakeholders

## Real-World Application: DreamWorks Animation Context

### Stakeholder Mapping Exercise
Create detailed profiles for each of the 13 departments:

#### Department Analysis Template
For each department, document:
1. **Primary Stakeholder**: Name, role, communication style
2. **Department Priorities**: What matters most to their success
3. **Current Pain Points**: Specific challenges with existing systems
4. **Success Metrics**: How they measure departmental performance  
5. **Technical Requirements**: Specific needs from the digital asset management system
6. **Influence Networks**: Who they trust and listen to
7. **Currency**: What motivates them (recognition, efficiency, quality, etc.)

### High-Impact Application Opportunities

#### 1. Monthly Cross-Department Alignment Sessions
- **Format**: 90-minute sessions with representatives from all 13 departments
- **Purpose**: Review progress, address conflicts, maintain alignment
- **Leadership Skills Applied**: Facilitation, consensus-building, trust maintenance
- **Success Metric**: Consistent attendance and productive outcomes

#### 2. Department Success Story Sharing
- **Format**: Regular presentations of wins and positive outcomes
- **Purpose**: Build momentum and demonstrate value across departments
- **Leadership Skills Applied**: Storytelling, buy-in building, influence through social proof
- **Success Metric**: Increased enthusiasm and support for initiatives

#### 3. Individual Stakeholder Strategy Sessions
- **Format**: Monthly one-on-one sessions with key stakeholders
- **Purpose**: Understand individual needs, build relationships, address concerns
- **Leadership Skills Applied**: Trust-building, influence without authority
- **Success Metric**: Strengthened relationships and individual stakeholder advocacy

#### 4. Cross-Department Collaboration Initiatives
- **Format**: Joint projects between departments that share common needs
- **Purpose**: Build natural alliances and demonstrate system benefits
- **Leadership Skills Applied**: Coalition building, consensus driving
- **Success Metric**: Successful collaborative projects and stronger inter-department relationships
