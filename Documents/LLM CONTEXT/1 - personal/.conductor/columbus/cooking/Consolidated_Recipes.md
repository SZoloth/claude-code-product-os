## Mexican & Southwest

### Southwest Chicken Burritos
*Makes 2 servings*

**Ingredients:**
- **Prepped Ingredients:**
  - [Blackened Seasoning](#blackened-seasoning)
  - [Cilantro-Lime Rice](#cilantro-lime-rice)
  - [Cilantro-Lime Crema](#cilantro-lime-crema)
  - [Black Bean Salsa](#black-bean-salsa)
- **Blackened Chicken:**
  - 1 large Chicken Breast, sliced in half lengthwise
  - Blackened Seasoning
  - Neutral Oil
- **For the Burritos:**
  - ½ cup Cilantro-Lime Rice
  - ½ cup Black Bean Salsa
  - 2 Burrito-size (large) Flour Tortillas
  - Cilantro-Lime Crema
  - Shredded Romaine Lettuce
  - Cheddar Cheese

**Instructions:**
1. **Prepare Chicken:**
   - Preheat the oven to 400ºF.
   - Season the chicken breast with generous pinches of blackened seasoning, covering both chicken halves.
   - Preheat a shallow frying pan on high heat with neutral oil.
   - Add seasoned chicken breast and sear for 4-5 minutes until golden. Flip and sear another 3-4 minutes. 
   - Transfer chicken to a sheet tray and finish cooking in the oven for 6-7 minutes (until 165ºF internal temperature).
   - Allow to rest for 5 minutes before cutting into small dice.

2. **Build the Burritos:**
   - Heat 1-2 tsp neutral oil in a pan over high heat.
   - Add cilantro-lime rice and reheat fully. Transfer to a bowl.
   - In the same pan, reheat black bean salsa and set aside.
   - Warm tortillas over medium-high flame or in a pan until soft (2-3 minutes).
   - Assemble by placing diced chicken, rice, bean salsa, crema, lettuce and cheese on each tortilla.
   - Fold the bottom up and over ingredients, tuck sides in, and roll closed.
   - Optional: Brown burritos in a pan with oil for 30 seconds per side.

### Crunchwrap Supremes
*Makes 2 servings*

**Ingredients:**
- **Prepped Ingredients:**
  - [Tinga Braising Sauce](#tinga-braising-sauce)
  - [Cilantro-Lime Crema](#cilantro-lime-crema)
- **For the Chicken:**
  - Neutral Oil
  - 3-4 Boneless Skinless Chicken Breasts
  - Kosher Salt
  - Tinga Braising Sauce
- **For the Crunchwraps:**
  - 3 Burrito-size (large) Flour Tortillas
  - Cheddar Cheese
  - 2 Tostadas
  - Cilantro-Lime Crema
  - Shredded Romaine Lettuce
  - 7-8 Cherry Tomatoes, halved

**Instructions:**
1. **Prepare the Chicken:**
   - Heat a large pot with neutral oil on high heat.
   - Pat chicken breasts dry and season with salt.
   - Sear chicken for 3-4 minutes. Flip and cover with tinga braising sauce.
   - Cover, reduce heat to low, and cook for 30 minutes until chicken falls apart easily.
   - Shred chicken using a stand mixer or forks.

2. **Prepare Crunchwraps:**
   - Cut 1 flour tortilla into 2 taco-sized rounds.
   - Heat the remaining 2 tortillas until soft (2-3 minutes).
   - Layer on a flat surface: shredded chicken, cheese, tostada, cilantro-lime crema, romaine lettuce, and tomatoes.
   - Top with the small tortilla rounds and fold edges of the large tortilla toward center, creating pleats.
   - Heat 1-2 tsp neutral oil in a skillet. Cook crunchwrap seam-side down until golden, then flip for 2-3 minutes.
   - Slice in half and serve.

**Note:** *This recipe makes a triple batch of chicken tinga to use in other recipes.*

### Taco Salad
*Makes 2 servings*

**Ingredients:**
- **Prepped Ingredients:**
  - Chicken Tinga (from [Crunchwrap Supremes](#crunchwrap-supremes) recipe)
  - [Black Bean Salsa](#black-bean-salsa)
  - [Chipotle Dressing](#chipotle-dressing)
  - [Cilantro-Lime Crema](#cilantro-lime-crema)
- **For the Salad:**
  - 1½ cups (2 servings) Chicken Tinga
  - 2 Burrito-sized (large) Flour Tortillas
  - Shredded Romaine Lettuce
  - Cherry Tomatoes, halved
  - Black Bean Salsa
  - Chipotle Dressing
  - Cilantro-Lime Crema
  - Cotija Cheese
  - Crispy Tortilla Strips

**Instructions:**
1. Reheat chicken tinga in a saucepan over medium-high heat until fully heated (about 8 minutes).
2. In a large mixing bowl, toss romaine, tomatoes, black bean salsa, cotija, chipotle dressing and a pinch of salt.
3. Serve salad topped with warm chicken tinga, crispy tortilla strips, and cilantro-lime crema.

### Elote Shrimp Tacos
*Makes 4-5 tacos*

**Ingredients:**
- **Prepped Ingredients:**
  - [Blackened Seasoning](#blackened-seasoning)
  - [Elote Pico](#elote-pico)
  - [Cilantro-Lime Rice](#cilantro-lime-rice)
- **Blackened Shrimp:**
  - 12-14 Medium Shrimp
  - Blackened Seasoning
  - 2-3 Tbsp Neutral Oil
- **For Assembly:**
  - 4-5 Corn Tortillas
  - Shredded Romaine Lettuce
  - Elote Pico
  - Lime Wedges
  - Cilantro-Lime Rice (on the side)

**Instructions:**
1. **Prepare Shrimp:**
   - Thaw shrimp under cold water if frozen and pat dry.
   - Season generously with blackened seasoning, coating fully.
   - Heat 2-3 Tbsp neutral oil in a large skillet over high heat.
   - Cook shrimp for 5-6 minutes, flipping halfway through, until pink and cooked through.

2. **Assemble Tacos:**
   - While shrimp cooks, combine romaine, pinch of salt, and lime juice in a bowl.
   - Warm corn tortillas directly over flame or in a pan until soft and pliable.
   - Layer lettuce mixture, 2-3 shrimp per taco, and top with elote pico.
   - Serve with lime wedges and cilantro-lime rice on the side.

### Burrito Bowls
*Makes 2 servings*

**Ingredients:**
- **Prepped Ingredients:**
  - Chicken Tinga (from [Crunchwrap Supremes](#crunchwrap-supremes) recipe)
  - [Chipotle Dressing](#chipotle-dressing)
  - [Cilantro-Lime Rice](#cilantro-lime-rice)
  - [Elote Pico](#elote-pico)
  - [Cilantro-Lime Crema](#cilantro-lime-crema)
- **For the Bowls:**
  - Remaining Chicken Tinga
  - 1 can Black Beans, drained and rinsed
  - Cilantro-Lime Rice
  - Elote Pico
  - Cotija Cheese
  - Shredded Romaine Lettuce
  - Cherry Tomatoes, halved
  - Chipotle Dressing
  - Cilantro-Lime Crema

**Instructions:**
1. Place chicken tinga in a saucepan on medium-high heat until reheated (about 8 minutes).
2. In a medium bowl, toss romaine, tomatoes, chipotle dressing, and a pinch of salt.
3. Plate cilantro-lime rice and top with black beans and chicken tinga.
4. Top with elote pico, cilantro-lime crema, dressed greens, and cotija cheese.

#### Elote Pico
*Makes about 1½ cups*

**Ingredients:**
- ½ large shallot, finely diced
- 6 oz (170 g) Fire-Roasted Corn
- ½ Bell Pepper, finely diced
- Neutral Oil
- 1½ tsp Blackened Seasoning
- Cilantro
- Cotija cheese
- Lime juice
- Jalapeño

**Instructions:**
1. Heat neutral oil in a pan over medium heat. Add shallot and cook until translucent (2-3 minutes).
2. Add fire-roasted corn, bell pepper, and a pinch of salt. Sauté until slightly softened (2-3 minutes).
3. Transfer to a mixing bowl and cool to room temperature.
4. Once cooled, add blackened seasoning, cilantro, cotija, lime juice, and jalapeño.
