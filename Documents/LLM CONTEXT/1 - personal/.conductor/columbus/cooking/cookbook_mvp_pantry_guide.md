# Cookbook‑MVP Pantry Guide
*Minimal viable stocklist + storage cheatsheet for: **Salt, Fat, Acid, Heat; Six Seasons; Gjelina; Run Fast Eat Slow; Field Notes; The Farm; Grains for Every Season; A Man & His Kitchen***

---
## TL;DR  
Lock in the items below and ~80 % of recipes across the stack stay cookable.  
**Weekly add‑ons:** 2‑3 seasonal veg, 1 leafy green, 1–2 fruits, 1 marquee protein.

---
## 1 · Shelf‑Stable Core (Pantry)
| Bucket | Keep On Hand | **Unopened** | **After Opening** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fats & Oils** | EVOO · avocado · coconut · ghee · shelf‑butter sticks | Cool, dark cabinet (≤ 70 °F) | Re‑cap tightly; EVOO/avocado good 6‑12 mo pantry; butter → fridge/freezer 1 mo/6 mo; coconut & ghee stable 9‑12 mo |
| **Acids** | Red, white‑wine, apple‑cider, rice, sherry vinegars · bottled & fresh lemon/lime juice · premium fish sauce | Dark pantry, indefinite | Vinegars still pantry; bottled citrus → fridge 6‑8 wks; fish sauce pantry 2 yrs (wipe rim) |
| **Salt & Umami** | Diamond kosher · flaky salt · tamari/low‑Na soy · white miso · oil‑packed anchovies · capers | Pantry, low humidity | Soy fridge 1 yr; miso fridge 1 yr+; anchovies fridge submerged in oil 2 mo; capers fridge brine‑covered 1 yr |
| **Spices (whole > ground)** | Peppercorns · chili flakes · cumin · coriander · fennel · smoked paprika · turmeric · cinnamon (stick & ground) · bay | Dark jar, < 1 yr for whole, < 6 mo ground | Same; toast & grind as needed for max punch |
| **Dried Herbs / Aromatics** | Oregano · thyme · rosemary · quality chili‑powder blend | Airtight jar, pantry 1 yr | Same; replace when aroma fades |
| **Grains & Pasta** | Sushi & basmati rice · farro/spelt · quinoa · steel‑cut oats · spaghetti + orecchiette · cornmeal/polenta | Airtight container, pantry 1 yr | Same; freeze 72 h first for bug insurance |
| **Legumes & Cans** | Chickpeas · cannellini · lentils · crushed tomatoes + paste · coconut milk · roasted peppers | Cool pantry, 2 yrs | Transfer half‑used cans to glass; fridge 5 days |
| **Baking & Sweeteners** | AP or 1:1 GF flour · baking powder · soda · brown sugar · maple · raw honey | Flour in airtight bin 6 mo (freeze for long) | Brown sugar airtight with clay disk; leaveners dry 6‑12 mo; honey indefinite |
| **Nuts & Seeds** | Almonds · walnuts · sesame · pepitas | Freezer 1 yr (best) or pantry 3 mo | Keep frozen; use straight from freezer |
| **Flavor Bombs** | Dijon · harissa/Calabrian chili · tomato‑paste tube · hot sauce | Pantry 1 yr | Fridge extends to 18 mo; tomato‑paste tube fridge 6 mo |

## 2 · Long‑Lived Fridge Staples
| Item | Storage & Life |
|---|---|
| Pastured **eggs** | Carton back of fridge ≤ 45 °F, 4–5 wks |
| **Parmesan wedge** | Cheese drawer, wrapped in wax/parchment + plastic, 6 mo |
| **Feta / chèvre** | Original brine / fresh brine jar, 2 wks |
| Plain **Greek yogurt** | Back of fridge, 2 wks past date |
| **Butter & ghee** | Butter: fridge 1 mo, freeze extras 6 mo · Ghee: pantry 9 mo |
| **White miso** | Tightly sealed jar, fridge 1 yr+ |
| **Carrots, celery, onions** | Crisper bags, high‑humidity drawer, 2–4 wks |
| **Garlic** | Mesh bag, cool dark spot, 3 mo |
| **Cabbage / kale** | Crisper, high humidity, 1–2 wks |
| **Lemons/Limes** | Fridge crisper, 3–4 wks |
| **Herbs (parsley, cilantro)** | Trim stems, jar water + bag tent, fridge 1 wk |

---
## 3 · Freezer MVP
| Stock | Best Practice |
|---|---|
| **Chicken / veg stock (ice cubes)** | Freeze flat in zip bags; label; 6 mo |
| **Salmon, shrimp, chicken thighs** | Vacuum‑seal or double bag; 6‑9 mo fish, 9 mo poultry |
| **Peas & spinach** | Reseal w minimal air; 8 mo |
| **Berries** | Single‑layer freeze then bag; 8 mo |
| **Bread / tortillas** | Slice first, double bag; 3 mo |

## 4 · Weekly Fresh‑Top‑Up Cheat‑Sheet
* 2–3 seasonal vegetables  
* 1 leafy green  
* 1–2 fruits  
* 1 marquee protein (beef, pork, or more fish)

_Add these to the MVP stocklist and you’re set for nearly any recipe in the collection._

---
## 5 · Workflow: Audit → Gap‑Buy → Label
1. **Audit** – Tick off what you own.  
2. **Gap‑Buy** – Add missing items to the next grocery order.  
3. **Label & Date** – Sharpie on freezer bags & opened jars; prevents cryptid condiments.

> **Pro‑Tip:** Freeze nuts for rancidity insurance, keep whole spices whole, and store oils away from stove heat for max shelf life.

Happy cooking!
