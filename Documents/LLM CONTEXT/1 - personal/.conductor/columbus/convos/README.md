## Purpose

This directory serves as a repository for:

- **Strategic conversations**: Recording important discussions that inform decision-making

- **Learning capture**: Preserving advice, perspectives, and wisdom shared in conversations

## Documentation Standards

### What to Include
- **Context**: Date, participants, and purpose of conversation
- **Key insights**: Main takeaways, advice, and perspectives shared
- **Action items**: Follow-up tasks or commitments made during conversation
- **Reflections**: Personal thoughts on the conversation's impact and relevance

### Quality Focus
- **Consent**: Only document conversations with appropriate permission
- **Accuracy**: Ensure transcripts and summaries accurately represent what was discussed
- **Privacy**: Respect confidential information and personal details
- **Value**: Focus on conversations that provide genuine learning or strategic value

## Best Practices

### During Conversations
- **Active listening**: Focus on understanding rather than just documenting
- **Permission**: Ask before recording or taking detailed notes
- **Follow-up**: Clarify important points and confirm understanding

### Post-Conversation
- **Prompt documentation**: Capture key points while memory is fresh
- **Action orientation**: Identify and act on commitments made
- **Gratitude**: Follow up with appreciation for time and insights shared
