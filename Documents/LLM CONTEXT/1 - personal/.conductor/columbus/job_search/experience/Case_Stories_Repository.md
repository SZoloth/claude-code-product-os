## 1. Echo Global Logistics - Transportation Management System Enhancement

### Situation
Echo Global Logistics, a third-party logistics provider specializing in freight brokerage, needed to improve their Transportation Management System (TMS) to better serve freight brokers and enhance operational efficiency. The existing system had gaps in user experience and competitive positioning.

### Task
- Conduct comprehensive competitive analysis of TMS platforms
- Research user needs and pain points through stakeholder interviews
- Develop strategic recommendations for product enhancement
- Create roadmap for improved freight broker experience

### Action
- **Competitive Analysis**: Analyzed 15+ TMS platforms including McLeod, MercuryGate, JDA, and emerging players
- **Stakeholder Research**: Conducted extensive interviews with freight brokers, operations teams, and end users
- **Market Analysis**: Evaluated pricing models, feature sets, and market positioning
- **Strategic Framework**: Developed comprehensive enhancement strategy focusing on:
  - User experience improvements
  - Advanced analytics and reporting
  - Integration capabilities
  - Mobile optimization
  - API development for third-party connectivity

### Result
- Delivered 51-page strategic analysis with actionable recommendations
- Identified key competitive differentiators and market opportunities
- Provided clear roadmap for TMS enhancement prioritization
- Enabled data-driven product development decisions

**Key Skills Demonstrated**: Competitive analysis, stakeholder research, product strategy, market analysis, logistics/transportation domain expertise

## 5. Xfinity/Comcast - Customer Service ROI Optimization

### Situation
Xfinity/Comcast needed to optimize customer service operations to reduce costs while improving customer experience, specifically targeting support call volume, truck roll efficiency, and service request handling.

### Task
- Build comprehensive ROI model for customer service improvements
- Analyze cost reduction opportunities across multiple service areas
- Project financial impact over 5-year timeline
- Develop business case for operational enhancements

### Action
- **Financial Modeling**: Built detailed ROI model analyzing:
  - Support call volume reduction (25% target decrease)
  - Truck roll optimization (25% reduction in incorrect/rescheduled visits)
  - Service request efficiency improvements
  - Self-service portal enhancement for upsells
- **Cost-Benefit Analysis**: Calculated baseline costs and projected savings across:
  - Annual support call costs: $1.89M to $9.45M baseline
  - Truck roll costs: $18K to $90K baseline
  - Service request handling costs
  - Revenue optimization through self-service upsells
- **5-Year Projections**: Developed comprehensive financial model showing:
  - Total cost savings: $840K to $4.2M annually
  - Additional revenue: $3.6K to $17.9K annually
  - Combined value creation: $858K to $4.29M annually

### Result
- Created detailed financial model demonstrating significant ROI potential
- Identified $4.29M in annual value creation opportunity by Year 5
- Provided data-driven business case for customer service optimization
- Established framework for measuring operational improvements

**Key Skills Demonstrated**: Financial modeling, ROI analysis, operational optimization, telecommunications domain expertise, business case development
