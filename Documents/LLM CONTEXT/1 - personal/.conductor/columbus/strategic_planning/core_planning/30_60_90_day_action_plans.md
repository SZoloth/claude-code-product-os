## Implementation Guidelines

### Daily Integration
- **Morning Planning**: Include strategic priority check in daily planning
- **Weekly Reviews**: Assess progress on 30-60-90 day goals
- **Monthly Check-ins**: Dedicated strategic progress review (30 minutes)

### Accountability Measures
- **Progress Tracking**: Weekly updates on key action items
- **Milestone Celebrations**: Recognize completion of major milestones
- **Course Correction Protocol**: Monthly assessment and adjustment process
- **External Accountability**: Share progress with Life Board of Directors

### Resource Allocation
- **Time**: 2-3 hours/week for strategic activities (beyond existing planning)
- **Financial**: Budget for skill development, training, networking events
- **Energy**: Schedule strategic work during peak energy times
- **Focus**: Limit to 2-3 major strategic initiatives at once
