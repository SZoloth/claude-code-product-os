*Scheduled for: September 30, 2025*

## Review Agenda

### 1. Vision Alignment Assessment (30 minutes)
- Review life vision document completion and refinement
- Assess how well current activities align with strategic direction
- Identify any vision adjustments needed based on Q3 experiences

### 2. Domain Progress Review (60 minutes)

#### Professional & Career (15 minutes)
- **Job Search Status**: Current pipeline, offers, timeline
- **Strategic Goal Progress**: Advancement toward target roles
- **Thought Leadership Development**: Blog posts, networking, speaking
- **Q4 Priorities**: Next quarter professional development focus

#### Health & Physical Performance (15 minutes)
- **Marathon Training Progress**: Adherence to plan, fitness improvements
- **October Marathon Preparation**: Race readiness, logistics
- **Post-Marathon Planning**: Recovery and future athletic goals
- **Q4 Priorities**: Training consistency, recovery protocols

#### Creative & Skill Development (15 minutes)
- **Priority Skills Progress**: Chosen creative skills development
- **Training System Effectiveness**: Application of marathon model to creative skills
- **Resource Allocation**: Budget utilization, training effectiveness
- **Q4 Priorities**: Skill development focus and community connection

#### Financial & Life Design (15 minutes)
- **Financial Strategy Implementation**: Budget adjustments, investment approach
- **Resource Allocation Review**: Supporting strategic priorities effectively
- **Life Design Support**: How finances enable vs constrain strategic goals
- **Q4 Priorities**: Financial optimization and strategic investments

### 3. Integration & Synergy Assessment (30 minutes)
- **Cross-Domain Connections**: How different life areas are reinforcing each other
- **Synergy Opportunities**: Identified and captured synergies
- **Integration Challenges**: Areas where domains conflict or compete
- **Strategic Adjustments**: Changes needed to improve integration

### 4. Relationship & Community Building (20 minutes)
- **Life Board of Directors Progress**: Advisory relationships established
- **Social Capital Development**: Network building and contribution activities
- **Community Engagement**: Hosting, teaching, mentoring progress
- **Q4 Priorities**: Relationship and community focus areas

### 5. Systems & Process Review (20 minutes)
- **Planning System Effectiveness**: Daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly planning
- **Accountability Measures**: What's working vs needs adjustment
- **Time & Energy Management**: Optimal allocation across priorities
- **Process Improvements**: Refinements for Q4 implementation
