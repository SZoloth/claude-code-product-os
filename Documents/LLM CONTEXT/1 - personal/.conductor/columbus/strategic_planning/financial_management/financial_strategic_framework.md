## Financial Decision-Making Framework

### Decision Criteria for Major Financial Choices
1. **10-Year Impact**: How does this decision affect wealth in 10 years?
2. **Cash Flow Effect**: Does this improve or hurt monthly cash flow?
3. **Tax Implications**: What are the tax consequences now and future?
4. **Opportunity Cost**: What am I giving up by making this choice?
5. **Life Alignment**: Does this support my broader life vision and goals?

### Quick Decision Guidelines
- **Purchases > $500**: 48-hour waiting period with decision framework
- **Subscriptions**: Quarterly review and justification required
- **Investment Changes**: Monthly evaluation with systematic approach
- **Career Moves**: Financial modeling of all options before deciding
