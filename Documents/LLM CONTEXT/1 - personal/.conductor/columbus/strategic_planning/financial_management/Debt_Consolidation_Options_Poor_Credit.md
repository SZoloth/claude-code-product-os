## Realistic Consolidation Options

### 1. Personal Loans for Poor Credit

#### **TOP RECOMMENDATION: Avant**
- **Minimum Credit Score**: 550 (you qualify)
- **APR Range**: 9.95% - 35.99%
- **Loan Amount**: Up to $35,000
- **Expected Rate for Your Profile**: 20-30% APR
- **Pros**: Most borrowers have 600-700 scores but accepts 580+
- **Cons**: Likely origination fee (up to 4.75%)

#### **SECOND CHOICE: Universal Credit**
- **Minimum Credit Score**: 580 (you qualify)
- **APR Range**: 11.69% - 35.99%
- **Same-day funding** after approval
- **Pros**: Quick funding, accepts poor credit
- **Cons**: Higher fees, variable approval criteria

#### **ALTERNATIVE: Upgrade**
- **Minimum Credit Score**: 580
- **APR Range**: 8.49% - 35.99%
- **Unique Feature**: Rate discount if they pay creditors directly
- **Pros**: Direct creditor payment option
- **Cons**: Strict underwriting

### Expected Terms for Your Profile
- **Loan Amount**: $20,000-25,000 (enough to cover Chase debt)
- **APR**: 20-35% (potentially better than 23.49%)
- **Term**: 2-7 years
- **Monthly Payment**: $400-600 depending on term
- **Origination Fee**: 3-8% of loan amount

## Alternative Options

### 2. Secured Balance Transfer Card

#### **Discover it® Secured Credit Card**
- **Requires**: Security deposit (becomes credit limit)
- **Balance Transfer Option**: Available at reduced rates
- **Pros**: More accessible for poor credit, deposit refundable
- **Cons**: Limited by deposit amount, won't get 0% APR

### 3. Credit Union Options
- **Local Credit Unions** often work with poor credit members
- **Typical Rates**: Better than online lenders (15-25% vs 25-35%)
- **Requirements**: May need to become member first
- **Process**: More personal, relationship-based lending

### 4. Debt Management Plan (DMP)
- **Provider**: Nonprofit credit counseling agency
- **Process**: They negotiate with Chase on your behalf
- **Expected Results**: 6-10% interest rate reduction (23.49% → 13-17%)
- **Monthly Fee**: $25-50
- **Timeline**: 3-5 year payoff plan
- **Credit Impact**: May show "enrolled in credit counseling" on report

## Backup Plans

### If All Consolidation Options Fail

#### Direct Chase Hardship Programs
- **Call Chase directly** and explain financial situation
- **Request hardship program** similar to what BofA offered
- **Potential outcomes**: Reduced APR, lower payments, settlement options
- **Precedent**: BofA offered you 6% without negotiation

#### 401(k) Loan (If Available)
- **Borrow up to 50%** of vested balance (max $50,000)
- **Interest goes back to your account** (typically 4-6%)
- **No credit check required**
- **Risk**: Full balance due if you lose job
- **Timeline**: Usually 2-4 weeks to funding

#### Strategic Payment Focus
- **Without consolidation**: Focus all extra payments on Chase debt
- **Avalanche method**: Pay minimums on everything else, attack 23.49% debt
