## Opportunity Evaluation Framework

### Financial Impact Assessment

#### Immediate Impact (0-12 months)
- **Income Generation**: How much additional income?
- **Cost Savings**: How much in reduced expenses?
- **Tax Benefits**: Immediate tax optimization impact?
- **Cash Flow**: Effect on monthly cash flow?

#### Long-Term Impact (1-10 years)
- **Compound Growth**: How does this compound over time?
- **Scalability**: Can this opportunity grow?
- **Exit Strategy**: How do you realize gains?
- **Portfolio Effect**: How does this affect overall allocation?

### Risk Assessment Matrix

#### Low Risk (High Probability of Success)
- FDIC-insured banking products
- Established investment strategies
- Proven business models
- Conservative debt management

#### Medium Risk (Moderate Probability, Higher Returns)
- Individual stock investments
- Real estate investment
- Side business development
- Career transition timing

#### High Risk (Lower Probability, Highest Returns)
- Startup investments
- Speculative investments
- New business ventures
- Market timing strategies

### Resource Requirements

#### Time Investment
- **Low**: <2 hours/week
- **Medium**: 2-5 hours/week
- **High**: 5+ hours/week

#### Financial Investment
- **Minimal**: <$500 setup cost
- **Moderate**: $500-5,000 setup cost
- **Significant**: $5,000+ setup cost

#### Knowledge Requirements
- **Basic**: Leverages existing knowledge
- **Moderate**: Requires some learning
- **Advanced**: Significant education needed
