## Investment Strategy During Job Search

### Conservative Investment Approach

#### Continue Automated Investing IF:
- Emergency fund is 8+ months of expenses
- Have unemployment benefits or bridge income
- Job search timeline is under 6 months
- High confidence in landing new role

#### Pause Additional Investing IF:
- Emergency fund is under 6 months
- No income replacement strategy
- Extended job search expected (6+ months)
- Uncertain about job market conditions

### Tax Optimization Opportunities

#### Lower Income Year Benefits
- **Roth IRA Conversions**: Convert traditional retirement funds at lower tax rates
- **Tax-Loss Harvesting**: Realize investment losses to offset future gains
- **Charitable Giving**: Bunch charitable deductions in higher income years
- **Business Expenses**: Maximize deductible job search expenses

#### Timing Considerations
- **Year-End Bonus**: Negotiate timing for tax optimization
- **Stock Option Exercise**: Consider tax implications of exercise timing
- **Start Date Negotiation**: Consider tax year impact of start date

## Risk Management During Career Transition

### Financial Risks and Mitigation

#### Extended Job Search Risk
**Risk**: Job search takes 6+ months
**Mitigation**: 
- 12-month emergency fund
- Active side income development
- Flexible living arrangements
- Conservative investment approach

#### Market Downturn Risk
**Risk**: Economic downturn affects job market
**Mitigation**:
- Recession-proof skill development
- Multiple industry targeting
- Enhanced emergency fund
- Network diversification

#### Compensation Stagnation Risk
**Risk**: New role doesn't provide expected increase
**Mitigation**:
- Multiple offer strategy
- Total compensation focus
- Long-term growth potential evaluation
- Side income as compensation supplement

### Insurance and Protection Strategy

#### Health Insurance Continuity
- **COBRA**: Temporary continuation of employer coverage
- **Marketplace Plans**: ACA marketplace options
- **Spouse Coverage**: If available through partner's employer
- **Short-Term Plans**: Bridge coverage for expected short transitions

#### Professional Insurance
- **Errors & Omissions**: If consulting during transition
- **General Liability**: For speaking/workshop activities
- **Disability Insurance**: Maintain coverage during transition
- **Life Insurance**: Ensure adequate coverage continues
