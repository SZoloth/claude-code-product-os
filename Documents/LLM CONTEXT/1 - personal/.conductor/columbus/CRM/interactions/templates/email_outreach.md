## Email Best Practices

### Subject Lines
- Keep under 50 characters when possible
- Be specific and actionable
- Avoid spam trigger words
- Include mutual connections when relevant
- Use value proposition for cold outreach

### Structure
1. **Opening**: Personal connection or context
2. **Body**: Clear purpose and value proposition
3. **Ask**: Specific, reasonable request
4. **Closing**: Professional and friendly sign-off

### Timing
- Send Tuesday-Thursday for professional emails
- Send morning (9-11am) or early afternoon (1-3pm)
- Avoid Mondays, Fridays, and holidays
- Follow up after 1 week if no response

### Personalization
- Research their recent work/posts
- Reference specific projects or achievements
- Connect to shared experiences or mutual connections
- Tailor value proposition to their current needs

### Follow-up Strategy
- First follow-up: 1 week
- Second follow-up: 2-3 weeks
- Third follow-up: 1-2 months
- Maximum: 3 follow-ups unless they respond
