**RO-DBT Practice → Rejection Handling**:
- Example: Turning toward discomfort with curiosity helps process job rejections

### Preventing Zero-Sum Thinking

**Potential conflict**: "If I focus on vision work Monday morning, I'm behind on DWA work"
**Reframe**: Vision work provides strategic clarity that makes DWA work more focused and effective. They reinforce each other. Plus, it's Monday rest day - high cognitive energy available.

**Potential conflict**: "Job search time takes away from Scout momentum"
**Reframe**: Job search is WHY I'm doing excellent DWA work (portfolio building). Scout success strengthens job search positioning. Positive-sum.

**Potential conflict**: "Marathon taper means less training time, so I should do more work"
**Reframe**: Taper CREATES cognitive energy for strategic work. Less physical training = more mental capacity. Integration opportunity, not trade-off.
