# Integrated Life Reviews

## Purpose

This folder contains weekly review and planning systems that integrate across ALL life domains - personal, professional, physical, emotional, and relational. The goal is to surface patterns, prevent strategic avoidance, and track progress in a way that builds self-respect through evidence of aligned action.

## Philosophy

**Integration Over Compartmentalization**: Life domains are interconnected, not siloed. Marathon training discipline supports job search consistency. DWA strategic thinking sharpens skills for future roles. Carter time recharges energy for everything else.

**Self-Respect Through Evidence**: Weekly reviews provide concrete evidence of becoming someone you respect, not just checking boxes or seeking external validation.

**Pattern Recognition Over Tactics**: Surface the deeper patterns (strategic avoidance, over-accommodation, recognition seeking) that shape outcomes across domains.

**Positive-Sum Thinking**: Look for synergies between domains rather than zero-sum trade-offs.

## System Components

### 1. Weekly Reviews (week-in-review/)
Comprehensive reflection on the past week across all life domains:
- Personal domain highlights (training, growth, relationship)
- Professional domain highlights (work wins, strategic progress)
- Integration insights (how domains supported/conflicted)
- Pattern recognition (coaching themes, blind spots surfaced)
- Self-respect metrics (evidence of aligned action)
- Gratitude across domains

**Naming Convention**: `YYYY-MM-DD-week-in-review.md` (dated for Sunday of week ending)

### 2. Weekly Planning (week-ahead/)
Forward-looking planning for the upcoming week:
- "Big Three" priorities across domains (not siloed)
- Energy/recovery mapping (marathon rest days = high cognitive energy)
- Strategic goal progress (vision work, job search, work outcomes)
- Accountability checkpoints
- Pattern interruption (what am I avoiding?)

**Naming Convention**: `YYYY-MM-DD-week-ahead.md` (dated for Monday of week starting)

### 3. Life Domains Dashboard
Living document tracking current state across all domains:
- Marathon training progress
- Job search metrics (Cycle 1: agency building)
- Work strategic load
- Relationship health
- Emotional/personal growth
- Vision work status

**File**: `life-domains-dashboard.md` (updated weekly)

### 4. Usage Guide
Instructions for weekly review and planning workflow.

**File**: `how-to-use-this-system.md`

- **strategic_planning/**: Long-term vision and quarterly planning (informed by weekly patterns)
- **marathon_training/**: Training logs and progress (integrated into weekly reviews)
- **job_search/**: Job search activities and progress (integrated into weekly reviews)

## Key Questions This System Answers

1. **Am I building self-respect or seeking external validation?**
2. **Where am I thinking zero-sum instead of positive-sum?**
3. **What patterns are repeating across weeks?**
4. **Am I executing tactics or building toward vision?**
5. **Which domains are reinforcing each other? Which are conflicting?**
6. **What am I avoiding by staying busy?**
7. **Is my energy foundation (sleep, health) supporting everything else?**

## Success Indicators

This system is working when:
- You can see clear patterns across weeks and months
- Strategic avoidance becomes immediately visible
- You celebrate progress without prompting
- Decision-making becomes values-aligned, not fear-driven
- Coaching insights integrate naturally into planning
- You feel integrated rather than compartmentalized
