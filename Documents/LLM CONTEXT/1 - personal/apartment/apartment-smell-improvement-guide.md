## Emergency Odor Situations

### Cooking Disasters
1. **Immediate**: Open all windows, turn on any fans
2. **Within 10 minutes**: Set up 3 bowls of white vinegar around kitchen
3. **Within 30 minutes**: Start emergency simmer pot (lemon + vanilla)
4. **Next day**: Replace vinegar bowls, extra baking soda treatment

### General Bad Smell Days
1. **Quick assessment**: Identify source room
2. **Ventilation blast**: Open windows in problem area + adjacent rooms
3. **Baking soda carpet treatment**: Generous sprinkle, let sit 2+ hours
4. **Essential oil refresh**: 2-3 sprays of appropriate scent for room

## DIY Recipes & Instructions

### Essential Oil Room Spray Recipe
**Ingredients:**
- 1 oz vodka or rubbing alcohol
- 8-10 drops essential oil (lavender for bedroom, citrus for kitchen/living room)
- 3 oz distilled water
- Glass spray bottle

**Instructions:**
1. Add alcohol to spray bottle
2. Add essential oils, swirl to mix
3. Add distilled water, shake well
4. Label with date (lasts 3 months)
5. Shake before each use

### Simmer Pot Recipes

**Basic Citrus Fresh (30 minutes active)**
- 2 cups water
- 2 lemon slices
- 1 tbsp vanilla extract  
- 2 sprigs rosemary

**Emergency Odor Buster (1 hour active)**
- 3 cups water
- 1 whole orange (sliced)
- 3 cinnamon sticks
- 5 whole cloves

**Calming Evening Blend (2 hours active)**
- 2 cups water
- 1 lemon (sliced)
- 3 sprigs thyme
- 1 tsp vanilla

### Closet Sachet System
**Materials:** 6 wool dryer balls, small containers
**Setup:** 
1. Add 2 drops essential oil per ball
2. Place 1 ball in each corner of closet
3. Store extras in airtight container
4. Refresh weekly with 1 drop oil

## Troubleshooting Guide

### "My plants are dying"
- **Too much water**: Let soil dry completely, reduce frequency
- **Too little light**: Move closer to window or try different room
- **Wrong plant for space**: Snake plants for low light, others need brighter spots

### "DIY sprays don't smell strong enough"
- **Increase oil**: Try 12-15 drops instead of 8-10
- **Fresh oils**: Replace oils older than 1 year
- **Better mixing**: Shake vigorously for 30 seconds before each use

### "Simmer pots create too much humidity"
- **Reduce time**: Try 30 minutes instead of hours
- **Open windows**: Crack window during simmering
- **Smaller batches**: Use 1 cup water instead of 2-3 cups

### "Baking soda isn't working"
- **More surface area**: Pour into shallow dishes instead of boxes
- **Replace more often**: Try every 2 weeks instead of monthly
- **Location matters**: Place directly in airflow paths

## Plant Selection & Care Guide

### Your First 3 Plants (Priority Order)

**#1 Snake Plant (Bedroom)**
- **Why**: Works overnight, tolerates neglect, low light OK
- **Where**: Bedroom corner, 3+ feet from bed
- **Care**: Water every 2-3 weeks, yellow leaves = overwatering
- **Cost**: $8-12, lasts years with minimal care

**#2 Spider Plant (Living Room)**  
- **Why**: Fastest air cleaning, produces baby plants, forgiving
- **Where**: Hanging or on plant stand, indirect bright light
- **Care**: Water weekly, trim brown tips, propagate babies
- **Cost**: $5-8, multiplies into many plants

**#3 Pothos (Kitchen)**
- **Why**: Handles kitchen humidity, trailing growth looks good, hardy
- **Where**: Top of refrigerator or high shelf, medium light
- **Care**: Water when soil dry 1 inch down, trim long vines
- **Cost**: $6-10, grows quickly
