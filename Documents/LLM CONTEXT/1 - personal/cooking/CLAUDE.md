# CLAUDE.md - Cooking & Meal Planning

## Folder Purpose

This folder contains comprehensive cooking resources, meal planning systems, and recipe collections designed to support efficient meal preparation and grocery management.

## Key Guidelines

- **Practical Focus**: All content should support actual meal preparation and cooking activities
- **System-Based**: Maintain organized systems for meal planning, grocery shopping, and recipe management
- **Efficiency-Oriented**: Optimize for time management and preparation efficiency
- **Health-Conscious**: Support nutritious meal choices aligned with marathon training and health goals

## Working with this Content

When working with files in this folder:

1. **Maintain Organization**: Keep recipes categorized and easily searchable
2. **Update Systems**: Regularly update meal planning templates and grocery lists
3. **Integration**: Connect meal planning with training schedules and energy needs
4. **Practical Testing**: Ensure recipes are tested and include proper instructions
