here are some tips and items for cooking and my kitchen:
- scrub high to low
- use rags instead of paper towels
- dirty laundry bin under the sink for used rags
- bar mats
- yakumi pans (bar caddy from webstraunt store or indian spice trays)
- more plastic squeeze bottles from jb prince
- black plastic gloves
- green and orange painters tape
- tools to right of stove
- silicon trivet
- keep a surface cleaner more visible
- hanging / magnetic storage 
- drip try in fridge shelf for "use first" shelf
- misen kitchen spatula
- cuisinart lemon squeezer
- stainless Y peeler
- stainless steel bench scraper 
- kitchen scissor (mac) 
- put a 1/8 sheet tray on a rightmost eye of your stove with a Bain Marie of tools, so they’re more readily available.
- keep "use 1st" pantry visible at all times (small containers for bulk things like rice oatmeal, etc)
- salt, sugar, butter accessible
- bus bin for handwash dishes or “I’ll get to it later but I still want to keep the sink clear” vibes?
- Surface sani is Nobel chemicals food contact surface sanitizer from @webstaurantstore
- glassware upside down on bar mat
- deli storage near fridge
- risers extend shelf space
- bar mat under esperesso machine
- fridge arrangement high to low: use first, veg, raw meat and eggs
- wet towel under slippery cutting board
- spoon/tongs for juicing lemons
