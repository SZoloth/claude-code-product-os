# Week 72 — Sunday Batch Prep Guide
*Designed for Sam’s 2.5 L pot, 7 L pot, stainless steel skillet, cast‑iron skillet, immersion blender, and standard sheet tray.*

## 0 – Set the Stage (5 min)
1. **Label six airtight containers**:
   - Crispy Shallots
   - Blackened Seasoning
   - Mango Salsa
   - Thai Vinaigrette
   - Risotto Base
   - Coconut Corn Soup
2. **Ice bath:** Large bowl ½‑filled with ice + water; park near sink.
3. **Tools within reach:** colander, sheet tray, spider, wooden spoon, ladle, knife, bench scraper, tape & marker.

---
## 1 – Produce Mise en Place (30 min active)
**Cut everything once; cook later.** Group same cuts to stay efficient.

| Produce           | Quantity  | Prep                                             |
| ----------------- | --------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Purple cabbage    | ½ head    | Shave thin ribbons (for tacos & bowls)           |
| Mangoes           | 2 large   | Peel; small dice (¼ in)                          |
| Bell peppers      | 2         | Remove seeds; small dice                         |
| English cucumber  | 1         | ½ small dice; ½ thin half‑moons                  |
| Carrots           | 2         | 1 roll‑cut (soup); 1 paper‑thin slices (salad)   |
| Jalapeño          | 1         | Mince (remove ribs for less heat)                |
| Corn on cob       | 2 ears    | Shuck; cut kernels off                           |
| Yukon Gold potato | 1 medium  | ¼ in dice                                        |
| Shallots          | 3 large   | 1 finely dice; 2 paper‑thin rings                |
| Red onion         | 1 small   | Finely dice                                      |
| Garlic            | 1 clove   | Smash & mince                                    |
| Ginger            | 1‑in knob | Peel; thin matchsticks                           |
| Cilantro          | 1 bunch   | Wash/dry; pick leaves; mince 2 Tbsp stems/leaves |

> **Storage:** Bag or deli‑cup each item; stack in fridge drawer.

---
## 2 – Long‑Cook Bases (Parallel, 35 min total)
### 2‑A Risotto Base (7 L pot)
1. Heat **2 Tbsp butter** + **1 Tbsp olive oil** med‑low.
2. Add **1 cup arborio rice**; toast 2 min, stirring.
3. Splash **¼ cup dry white wine**; cook off.
4. Add **3½ cups warm chicken stock** 1 ladle at a time, stirring every 2–3 min (≈ 18 min). Keep at gentle simmer.
5. When rice is al dente & creamy, stir in **¼ cup grated Asiago** + **½ tsp salt**.
6. Spread on sheet tray; flash‑cool 5 min; transfer to labeled container → fridge.

### 2‑B Coconut Corn Soup (2.5 L pot)
1. While risotto cooks, add to small pot:
   - **1 tsp avocado oil**
   - minced garlic + ginger
2. Sauté 1 min; add diced potato & corn kernels. Sweat 3 min.
3. Pour **1 can (13.5 oz) coconut milk** + **1 cup veggie stock**.
4. Toss in roll‑cut carrot, **½ tsp salt**, **¼ tsp black pepper**.
5. Simmer 20 min until potato tender.
6. Off heat → immersion‑blend until silky. Taste; adjust salt. Chill in ice bath 10 min, then container → fridge.

---
## 3 – Quick Wins While Pots Bubble
### 3‑A Crispy Shallots (stainless skillet)
1. Heat **½ cup neutral oil** med‑low; add shallot rings.
2. Fry, stirring, 6‑8 min until deep‑golden.
3. Lift with spider → paper towel. Salt lightly. Cool & store ROOM TEMP in sealed jar.
4. Strain oil → jar (future sauté).

### 3‑B Blackened Seasoning
In ramekin whisk:
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp cayenne
- ½ tsp black pepper
Label & seal.

---
## 4 – Chow Mein Noodles (15 min)
1. Bring fresh water in 2.5 L pot to rolling boil.
2. Drop **6 oz chow‑mein noodles**; cook 3 min.
3. Drain; rinse cold; toss with **1 tsp sesame oil**. Chill & portion for Days 2 & 5.

---
## 5 – Cold Prep Finishes
### 5‑A Mango Salsa
Combine in bowl:
- diced mango, bell pepper, cucumber (diced half)
- 2 Tbsp minced cilantro, minced jalapeño
- juice 1 lime, **¼ tsp salt**
Toss; seal; refrigerate.

### 5‑B Thai‑Inspired Vinaigrette (blender)
Blend until smooth:
- 2 Tbsp natural peanut butter
- 2 Tbsp rice vinegar (sub = white wine vinegar + squeeze lime)
- 1 Tbsp soy sauce or tamari
- 1 Tbsp lime juice
- 1 Tbsp honey
- 1 tsp fish sauce
- 2 Tbsp water (thin to pourable)
Bottle & chill.

---
## 6 – Protein Prep
### 6‑A Thai Curry‑Marinated Chicken
1. In bowl: **2 Tbsp green curry paste** + **1 Tbsp avocado oil** + zest & juice ½ lime.
2. Add **1 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs**; toss.
3. Cover → fridge (≥ 1 hr; up to 48 hr).

### 6‑B Seafood Portioning
- Shrimp (30‑34 count): portion **½ lb** for tacos, **½ lb** for bowls.
- Mahi‑Mahi: portion **2 fillets (6 oz each)** for Blackened Tuesday.
Pat dry; store in separate sealed containers on lowest shelf.

---
## 7 – Cool, Pack, Clean (≤ 20 min)
1. Verify risotto & soup are < 70 °F; lid & refrigerate.
2. Salsa, vinaigrette, noodles → fridge; seasoning & shallots → pantry.
3. Drain & wipe skillet; wash pots, knives, board.
4. Clear counters; compost scraps; *Done!* 💪
