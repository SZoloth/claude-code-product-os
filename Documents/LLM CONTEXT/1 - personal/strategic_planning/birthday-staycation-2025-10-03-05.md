## Recipes

### Saturday Dinner

**Apple-Cheddar Grilled Cheese**
- Good sourdough or brioche bread, sliced
- Sharp cheddar cheese (or cheddar+gruyère blend)
- Apple slices (Honeycrisp or Opal), thinly sliced
- Butter for griddling
- Optional: Dijon mustard, apple butter

**Instructions:**
1. Butter one side of each bread slice
2. On the non-buttered side, layer cheese, thin apple slices, more cheese
3. Optional: spread Dijon or apple butter on inside of top slice
4. Griddle on medium heat until golden and cheese is molten (3-4 min per side)
5. Serve immediately with tomato soup

**Tomato Soup**
- Use good-quality canned or carton tomato soup, heated per instructions
- Optional: add a drizzle of cream or fresh basil

### Dessert Options

**Option 1: Apple Crisp (from scratch)**
- 6-8 apples, peeled and sliced
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- ¼ cup granulated sugar
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- ¾ cup rolled oats
- ¾ cup all-purpose flour
- ¾ cup light brown sugar
- ½ cup butter, cold and cubed
- Pinch of salt

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 375°F
2. Toss sliced apples with lemon juice, granulated sugar, and cinnamon
3. Spread in buttered baking dish
4. Mix oats, flour, brown sugar, and salt
5. Cut in cold butter until crumbly
6. Sprinkle topping over apples
7. Bake 35-40 minutes until golden and bubbly
8. Serve warm with ice cream or Creami creation

**Option 2: Store-bought apple pie**
- Warm per package instructions
- Serve with ice cream or Creami creation

### Ninja Creami Recipes (Freeze 24 hours ahead)

**Vanilla-Honey Ice Cream (for apple crisp/pie)**
- 240ml (1 cup) whole milk
- 240ml (1 cup) heavy cream
- 60g (¼ cup) granulated sugar
- 2 tbsp non-fat milk powder (optional, for texture)
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt
- Honey for drizzling

**Instructions:**
1. Whisk all ingredients (except honey) until sugar dissolves
2. Pour into Creami pint container, freeze 24 hours
3. Process on Ice Cream setting
4. Create well in center, drizzle 1-2 tbsp honey
5. Re-spin to incorporate

**Apple-Cider Sorbet (vegan option)**
- 355ml (1.5 cups) unfiltered apple cider
- 2 tbsp granulated sugar
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- ¼ tsp ground cinnamon
- Pinch of salt

**Instructions:**
1. Whisk all ingredients until sugar dissolves
2. Pour into Creami pint container, freeze 24 hours
3. Process on Sorbet setting
4. Serve immediately or store in freezer

**Pumpkin-Cheesecake Light (bonus option)**
- 170g (¾ cup) Greek yogurt or skyr, plain
- 120ml (½ cup) whole milk
- 100g (scant ½ cup) pumpkin purée
- 2 tbsp maple syrup
- ¼ tsp pumpkin pie spice
- Pinch of salt
- Graham crackers for topping (optional)

**Instructions:**
1. Whisk all ingredients until smooth
2. Pour into Creami pint container, freeze 24 hours
3. Process on Lite Ice Cream setting
4. Top with crumbled graham crackers if desired

### Fall Atmosphere Elements

**Stovetop Simmer-Pot**
- Apple peels (save from apple crisp/pie prep)
- Orange peels
- 3-4 cinnamon sticks
- 1 tbsp whole cloves
- 2-3 star anise
- Fresh rosemary sprigs (optional)
- Water to cover

**Instructions:**
1. Combine all ingredients in a pot
2. Bring to a simmer, then reduce to low
3. Keep simmering throughout evening, adding water as needed
4. Creates amazing fall aroma throughout apartment

**Spiced Apple Cider (for thermos)**
- Unfiltered apple cider
- Mulling spices (cinnamon stick, cloves, star anise, orange peel)
- Heat gently, strain, pour into Tervis tumbler
