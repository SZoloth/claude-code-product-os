1. Core Philosophy

Megan Hellerer re-frames life design as a scientific method for living: form a directional hypothesis, run low-stakes experiments, gather data, iterate. Success is redefined from hitting a fixed destination to remaining in continuous aligned motion. The method prizes agency, openness to timing/luck (e.g., AOC’s unexpected congressional call), and somatic wisdom over purely cognitive reasoning. 

2. Primary Frameworks & Tools

a. Directional vs. Destinational Thinking

• Destinational = linear plans, certainty worship, external timelines (“by 30 I should…”)
• Directional = next right step, tolerance of ambiguity, curiosity as GPS, headlights-distance planning only. 

b. Four Omens Checklist
 1. Obligation/Shoulds – external conventions.
 2. Objectivity – “It’s the logical move” without inner resonance.
 3. Optics – How it will look to others.
 4. Outcomes – Decision made solely for a predicted payoff.
Presence of any omen ⇒ pause or reroute. 

c. Warm-or-Colder Calibration
 1. Recall one deeply aligned past choice → note bodily sensations (warm).
 2. Recall one misaligned choice → note sensations (cold).
 3. Use this personal legend to grade every micro- and macro-decision. 

d. Aligned Action Formula (AA-Formula)
 1. Screenshot the spark/desire.
 2. Say it aloud or journal it.
 3. Research (Google, GPT, books).
 4. Conversation with someone living it.
 5. Action – take one tolerable, reversible step.
Loop until clarity or cooling. 

e. Haywalts Inventory

Catalog chronic frictions you’ve normalized (toxic boss, health issue, leaky faucet). Remove one per week to clear cognitive bandwidth for directional work. 

3. Decision Heuristics & Nuances

• Time-Waste Reframe: Exploration is never wasted if directionally right—failed experiments still add truthful data.
• Discomfort ≠ Misalignment: Hard, vulnerable choices may still be warm if they carry freedom/relief on the far side (e.g., rewriting a book).
• Life Is Short and Long: Too short to stay in known misalignment; long enough to run multiple life experiments sequentially.
• Somatic > Cognitive: Thinking mind hosts fear and “4 O” chatter; sensing mind bypasses them.
