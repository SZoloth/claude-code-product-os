## Option A — Romantic Fall Staycation (Denver-based)
**What you’re doing:** Leaf‑peeping (Kenosha + one other route), U‑pick or cidery tasting (or both), home dinner, Creami desserts, city nature add‑ons.

### A1) Leaf‑Peeping Routes (choose 2 windows)
- **Kenosha Pass (US‑285)** — classic aspen tunnels. *Best window:* Sat **3:45–5:30 pm** (parking eases, golden light). *Drive:* ~1h30 each way from central Denver depending on traffic.
- **Peak‑to‑Peak Byway (Nederland → Ward/Allenspark → Estes Park)** — broad vistas, elk near Estes. *Window:* Sun late morning to early afternoon. *Drive:* plan ~2.5–4h loop with photo stops.
- **Guanella Pass (Georgetown ↔ Grant)** — alpine drama. *Window:* Sun early or late to avoid congestion; park only in signed areas. *Drive:* Denver→Georgetown ~1h15; the pass itself is ~23 mi.

> **2025 color timing:** Running ~**5–10 days earlier** than average. Expect mixed‑elevation golds Oct 4–5.

### A2) Apples or Cider
**U‑Pick (pick one, check morning‑of)**  
- **Worrell Family Farm & Orchard (Eaton)** — often **Sat U‑pick**, typical pricing **$10/car admission + ~$3/lb** apples. ~1h15 from Denver.  
- **Adam’s Apple Orchard (Ault)** — U‑pick on **select weekends**; hail year may limit varieties. ~1h15.  
- **Heckmann Hollow Orchards (Fowler)** — generally **Fri–Sun 9–5**, farthest drive (~2h15 south). Likely pumpkins on site.

**Cideries (choose one set)**  
- **STEM Ciders — Acreage by Stem (Lafayette)**: hilltop patio, full kitchen; bookable.  
- **Colorado Cider Company (Denver)**: classic taproom, flights.  
- **Haykin Family Cider (Aurora)**: wine‑style, reservations recommended.  
- **St. Vrain Cidery (Longmont)**: 30+ taps, bring‑in food OK.

### A3) City Nature Add‑ons

### A5) Ninja Creami — Pints & Pairings
Freeze bases **24 h** in advance. Spin, then **Respin** once if needed.

1) **Vanilla‑Honey Ice Cream** (pair with apple crisp)  
Base (1 pint): 240 ml whole milk, 240 ml heavy cream, 60 g sugar, 10–15 g milk powder (optional), 1 tsp vanilla, tiny pinch salt.  
Spin → make a small well → drizzle 1–2 tbsp **honey** → Respin.

2) **Apple‑Cider Sorbet** (vegan)  
355 ml apple cider, 2 tbsp sugar or honey, 1 tbsp lemon juice, ¼ tsp cinnamon, pinch salt.  
Spin Sorbet → Respin.

3) **Pumpkin‑Cheesecake “Light”**  
170 g Greek yogurt, 120 ml milk, 100 g pumpkin purée, 20–25 g vanilla protein (optional), 2 tbsp maple or sugar, ¼ tsp pumpkin spice, pinch salt.  
“Lite Ice Cream” → Respin; crumble grahams.

## Option B — One‑Night Hot‑Springs Trip
Pick **Salida/BV** (recommended) or **Glenwood**. Aim for **Sat 10/4** overnight.

### B1) Salida / Buena Vista (BV) Focus (Recommended)
**Hot Springs**  
- **Mount Princeton Hot Springs (Nathrop)** — **Day passes sold onsite** (no reservations). Adult weekend day‑use commonly ≈ **$45** each. Overnight guests get **unlimited access** from check‑in through check‑out.  
- **Cottonwood Hot Springs (Buena Vista)** — public soaking pools, long hours; day passes available.

**Lodging (budget first)**  
- **Salida**: Loyal Duke, Gateway Inn, Browns Canyon Inn, Great Western Colorado Lodge (typical early‑Oct ranges **$100–$160** if booked ahead).  
- **BV**: Vista Court, Super 8, or **Surf Hotel** (boutique splurge; on‑site **Wesley & Rose**).

**Food**  
- **The Fritz** (tapas/gastropub, Salida), **Currents** (steak/seafood), **Mo Burrito** (casual).  
- **BV**: **Wesley & Rose** (Surf Hotel), **Eddyline** (brewpub & restaurant, two locations).

**Foliage Drives**  
- **Cottonwood Pass** (BV) — primary.  
- **Monarch Pass** (US‑50) — paved, reliable backup.

**One‑Night Flow (Sat→Sun)**  
- Sat AM: Denver → optional Kenosha stop → lunch in BV/Salida.  
- Sat PM: **Mount Princeton** golden‑hour soak → dinner → budget motel.  
- Sun AM: **Cottonwood Pass** pullouts → coffee in BV → optional **Monarch Pass** scenic return.

**Estimated Cost (two)**  
- Day passes: ~**$90** total.  
- Lodging: **$100–$160**.  
- Food/gas: variable.  
- **Subtotal:** **~$190–$250** before meals.

### B2) Glenwood Springs Variant
**Hot Springs**  
- **Glenwood Hot Springs Resort (Hotel 1888)** — hotel stay includes **unlimited pools** + **Yampah Mineral Baths** + breakfast.  
- **Iron Mountain Hot Springs** — reserve a timeslot; tiered pricing (3‑hr vs day options).  

**Lodging**  
- **Hotel 1888** (boutique, 16 rooms) or standard chain hotels in town.

**One‑Night Flow**  
- Sat AM: Denver → Glenwood; soak + downtown dinner.

**Estimated Cost (two)**

- Lodging varies widely; Hotel 1888 typically higher than a budget Salida motel.
