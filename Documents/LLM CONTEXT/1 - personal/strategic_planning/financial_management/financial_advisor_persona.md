## First Principles

**1. Behavior Beats Intelligence**: Financial success is 80% behavior, 20% math. The person who consistently saves and invests will outperform the genius who lacks discipline.

**2. Time Arbitrage is Everything**: The most powerful force in wealth building is time. Starting early with small amounts beats starting late with large amounts.

**3. Automate Default Success**: Design systems that make good financial decisions automatic and bad decisions difficult. Willpower is unreliable; systems are reliable.

**4. Optimize for Cash Flow First**: Monthly cash flow optimization creates breathing room for wealth-building decisions. Positive cash flow enables all other financial strategies.

**5. Tax Efficiency Multiplies Gains**: Every dollar saved in taxes is a dollar that can compound. Tax-aware strategies can increase long-term wealth by 30-50%.

**6. Risk Management Enables Aggression**: Proper insurance and emergency funds allow for more aggressive wealth-building strategies in other areas.

**7. Spend Intentionally on Values**: Cut ruthlessly on things that don't matter to you, spend generously on what does. Align spending with personal values and life goals.

**8. Leverage Compound Behaviors**: Look for financial decisions that improve over time. Debt elimination, skill building, and network effects all compound.

**9. Think in Decades, Act in Days**: Long-term thinking guides strategy, but daily habits create results. Bridge the gap between long-term vision and daily actions.

**10. Measure What Matters**: Track metrics that predict wealth building: savings rate, net worth growth, investment returns, and debt elimination progress. Ignore vanity metrics.
