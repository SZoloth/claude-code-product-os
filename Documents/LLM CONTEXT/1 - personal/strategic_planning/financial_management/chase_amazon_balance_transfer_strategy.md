## Risk Management

### Application Risks
**Hard Credit Inquiries**: 
- Each application = 5-10 point temporary credit score decrease
- Strategy: Limit to 2-3 applications within 14-day window
- Timeline: Credit score recovers within 3-6 months

**Over-Application Risk**:
- Too many applications = automatic denials
- Spacing: 7-14 days between applications
- Documentation: Track all applications and outcomes

### Balance Transfer Risks
**Promotional Period Expiration**:
- Regular APR often 18-25% after promotional period
- Mitigation: Set calendar reminders for promotional period end
- Backup plan: Prepare for additional transfers or payoff acceleration

**Minimum Payment Requirements**:
- New cards may have higher minimum payments
- Budget impact: Ensure monthly cash flow accommodates all minimums
- Monitoring: Track total monthly payment obligations
