## Framework Philosophy

### Systems Thinking
- **Holistic optimization**: Frameworks consider interactions between different life systems and domains
- **Energy as currency**: Recognition that energy management is as important as time management
- **Sustainable practices**: Systems designed for long-term effectiveness rather than short-term optimization

### Strategic Integration
- **Top-down alignment**: Operational systems serve broader strategic objectives
- **Bottom-up feedback**: Daily operational insights inform strategic planning refinements
- **Cross-domain synergy**: Frameworks identify opportunities where improvements in one area benefit others

## Core Framework Elements

### Daily Strategic Integration
- **Priority alignment**: Systematic connection between daily actions and strategic objectives

### Energy Optimization
