# Vinalhaven Transportation & Logistics Guide

## Brown's Boatyard Water Taxi - PRIMARY SERVICE
**Phone:** 207-867-4621  
**Hours:** 7AM-5PM, Mon-Sat (NO SUNDAY SERVICE)  
**Booking:** MUST call ahead or arrive at boatyard before desired departure time

### Pricing
- **One-way:** $8/person
- **Round-trip:** $10/person  
- **Bikes:** $4/bike (game-changer for North Haven)

### Route & Timing
- **Pickup:** Brown's Boatyard (north side of Vinalhaven)
- **Dropoff:** VH Public Dock (25min drive from downtown Vinalhaven)
- **Schedule:** On-the-hour departures (7am, 8am, 9am, etc.)
- **Key limitation:** No trips without confirmed passengers

## Strategic Uses for Crockett's Cove Residents

### Day Trips to North Haven
- **Morning departure:** 8am or 9am with bikes
- **Explore:** North Haven trails, Pulpit Harbor area
- **Return:** 3pm or 4pm
- **Cost:** $10 + $4 bike = $14 per person roundtrip

### Supply Runs
- **Advantage:** Avoid Rockland drive + Maine State Ferry
- **Target:** North Haven General Store, marine supplies
- **Timing:** Mid-morning out, early afternoon back

### Mainland Connections
- **Option 1:** Water taxi to North Haven → drive 25min to VH downtown → Maine State Ferry
- **Option 2:** Direct boat charter (explore other services)

## Seasonal Considerations
- **Summer:** Book early, high demand
- **Winter:** Service may be weather-dependent
- **Sunday gap:** Plan around no-service day
