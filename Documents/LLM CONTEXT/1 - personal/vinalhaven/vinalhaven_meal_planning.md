## RECIPES

### Martha Stewart's Perfect Lobster Roll
*Serves 4 • Prep: 15 min • Cook: 15 min*

**Ingredients:**
- Two 2½-lb or three 1½-lb live lobsters
- ⅓ cup mayonnaise  
- 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 1 tsp coarse salt
- 4 top-split hot dog buns
- Melted unsalted butter for brushing
- Lemon wedges & potato chips for serving

**Method:**
1. Steam lobsters in large pot w/ steamer basket, 1" water boiling. Drop lobsters headfirst, cover, steam 12-14 min until bright red. Check doneness: antenna pulls off easily.
2. Extract meat from shells (claws, tails, legs), coarsely chop.
3. Mix mayo, lemon juice, salt in bowl. Fold in lobster meat.
4. Brush tops & sides of buns with butter. Toast in cast iron skillet, flipping once, ~3 min until golden.
5. Stuff with lobster mixture, serve immediately.

**Variations:**
- Maryland Spice: + Old Bay seasoning
- California Cool: + lime juice, avocado, cilantro  
- Connecticut Bright: + melted butter, orange juice, tarragon

**VH Notes:** Hit up whatever boat's selling off Carver's Harbor — fresh beats shipped every time. If using pre-cooked meat (no shame), just need ~1 lb picked meat.
