# Podcast & Audiobook Recommendations

*Created: August 12, 2025*

## Philosophy & Approach

**The Culture-Stack PM Principle**: Barbell curiosity (weird + practical), default-alive calendars, and "one good source beats twenty summaries." Focus on compounding knowledge over entertainment.

## Audiobook Queue (High-Leverage First)

### Wealth/Business
- **The Almanack of Naval Ravikant** (Eric Jorgenson) — Leverage/wealth thinking; crisp audio edition
- **The Psychology of Money** (Morgan Housel) — Behavior > spreadsheets; re-listen annually
- **Richer, Wiser, Happier** (William Green) — Investor mental models with unusual humility
- **The Simple Path to Wealth** (JL Collins) — Index-fund operating system

### Performance/Training
- **Endure** (Alex Hutchinson) — Limits, pacing, fueling; marathon brain candy
- **Good to Go** (Christie Aschwanden) — Recovery science sans snake oil
- **The Inner Game of Tennis** (W. Timothy Gallwey) — Performance psychology for life

### Cooking/Lifestyle
- **An Everlasting Meal** (Tamar Adler) — Batch cooking as philosophy
- **Kitchen Confidential** (Anthony Bourdain, read by AB) — Vibe + palate; pure joy in audio

### Literature that Changes How You Think
- **Klara and the Sun** (Kazuo Ishiguro) — AI consciousness through literary lens
- **The Left Hand of Darkness** (Ursula K. Le Guin) — Gender, power, otherness
- **Invisible Cities** (Italo Calvino) — Imagination + urban planning poetry

### Philosophy/Ideas
- **The Righteous Mind** (Jonathan Haidt) — Moral psychology, essential for leadership
- **Ways of Seeing** (John Berger) — How we look at art/world, visual literacy
- **The Dawn of Everything** (Graeber & Wengrow) — Rethinks human organization

### Cultural/Historical
- **The Spell of the Sensuous** (David Abram) — Ecology meets phenomenology
- **The Art of Not Being Governed** (James C. Scott) — Power, states, freedom

## Weekly Listening Schedule

### Sample Week Structure
- **Monday run**: Latent Space (technical grounding)
- **Tuesday commute**: Ezra Klein Show (big ideas)
- **Wednesday chores**: Song Exploder (aesthetic training)
- **Thursday workout**: Strength Running (performance)
- **Friday evening**: Entitled Opinions (intellectual stretch)
- **Weekend long run**: Rotate literary audiobooks

### Cross-Pollination Strategy
Look for episodes that bridge interests:
- Ezra Klein interviewing AI researchers about consciousness
- 99% Invisible on urban planning (connects to travel/design interests)
- Philosophy Talk on ethics of technology leadership

## Micro-System for Consistency

### Subscription Management
- Auto-subscribe to maximum 5 shows
- Everything else = "check when relevant"
- Ruthless unsubscribe if not adding value

### Listening Habits
- Listen at 1.2–1.4× speed
- Skip after 7 minutes if not hitting
- Keep one audiobook always "active" for long runs/chores

### Review Process
- Weekly: Assess what's working/not working
- Monthly: Rotate in new shows, retire stale ones
- Quarterly: Update audiobook queue based on current goals

## Success Metrics
- Building a listening diet that compounds rather than just entertains
- Each input should either deepen existing knowledge or create unexpected connections
- Consistent engagement rather than binge consumption
- Ability to draw insights across disciplines in conversations and decision-making

## Notes
- Focus on skin-in-the-game hosts (practitioners, not just pundits)
- Prioritize deep archives with timeless episodes
- Balance 70/30 evergreen content vs. news/current events
- Optimize for different contexts: runs, chores, commute, wind-down
