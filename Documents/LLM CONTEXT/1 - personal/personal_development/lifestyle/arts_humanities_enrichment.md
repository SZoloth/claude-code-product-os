# Arts & Humanities Enrichment Plan

*Created: August 12, 2025*

## Goal
Deepen engagement with arts and humanities to maintain intellectual curiosity, stay quirky, and have richer experiences while avoiding the trap of purely utilitarian thinking.

## Current Cultural Elements (Audit)
- **Design/Aesthetics**: Interior design principles show visual literacy
- **Music**: Jazz clubs and Nocturne Jazz Supper - some music appreciation  
- **Performance**: Gothic Theater - live performance interest
- **Museums**: Mentioned in date ideas but not developed
- **Travel**: Multiple international destinations planned with cultural potential

## Strategies for Maintaining Quirky Intellectual Curiosity

### Cross-Pollination Method
Connect existing interests (fitness, product leadership, personal development) with humanities:
- Study philosophy of ancient athletic competitions
- Read about Japanese design principles affecting product thinking
- Explore psychology behind great literature
- Research how different cultures approach leadership and teamwork

### Contrarian Reading
Deliberately seek viewpoints that challenge current thinking:
- Read books/articles that argue against common wisdom in your field
- Explore philosophical schools that contradict your worldview
- Seek out perspectives from different cultural backgrounds
- Question assumptions about productivity, success, and fulfillment

### Weekly Intellectual Sabbath
Dedicate one evening per week to purely curiosity-driven exploration:
- No productivity goals or practical outcomes required
- Follow interesting intellectual threads wherever they lead
- Allow for meandering through Wikipedia, documentaries, or books
- Document unexpected connections and insights

## Approaches for Richer Cultural Experiences

### Leverage Planned Travel
Transform upcoming trips into deep cultural immersion:
- **Mexico City**: Learn basic Spanish, read one novel by Mexican author beforehand, seek out local cultural practices beyond tourist sites
- **Portugal**: Study Portuguese history and literature, explore fado music tradition
- **Japan**: Read about Japanese aesthetics, philosophy, and social customs before visiting
- **General**: For each destination, commit to one cultural learning goal beyond sightseeing

### Denver Cultural Circuit
Build systematic approach to local cultural offerings:

#### Museums & Galleries (Systematic Monthly Rotation)
**January-March Focus: Major Institutions**
- **Denver Art Museum**: Kent Monkman exhibition (through Aug 17) - challenges thinking about history, queerness, environmental justice
- **Clyfford Still Museum**: Single-artist focus creates deep aesthetic immersion
- **MCA Denver**: "Selections from Prospect.6 New Orleans" (May-Aug) - cutting-edge contemporary voices

**April-June Focus: Gallery District**
- **Robischon Gallery**: Museum-quality shows curated by Jennifer Doran
- **Art District on Santa Fe**: First Friday walks (monthly) + Third Friday openings
- **CU Experience Gallery**: Emerging/experimental work, recently found its voice

#### Literary & Bookstore Circuit
**Independent Bookstore Rotation:**
- **Second Star to the Right** (Pearl Street): Monthly author events, voices outside usual reading
- **Spicy Librarian** (RiNo): Denver's new romance bookstore - different genre approaches to human psychology
- **Tattered Cover** (Colfax): Institution with regular author talks and discussion groups

**Must-Attend Literary Events:**
- **Lighthouse Writers Lit Fest** (June 6-13): Craft workshops and author readings
- **Readers Take Denver** (2025): 300+ authors, panels, workshops - intensive cultural immersion
- **DPL Spotlights Series**: Free monthly author talks at Denver Public Library

#### Theater & Performance
**Systematic Theater Approach:**
- **DCPA Season Pass**: Regular exposure to Broadway-quality productions
- **"A Christmas Carol"** (Nov 21-Dec 28): Denver cultural tradition
- **Smaller Venues**: Alternate between DCPA and experimental spaces

#### Music & Cultural Events
**Annual Cultural Anchors:**
- **Denver Arts Week** (Nov 7-16, 2025): City-wide cultural celebration
- **Denver Film Festival** (Oct 31-Nov 9): Largest in region - international perspectives
- **Red Rocks** experiences: Concerts, sunrise yoga, lectures, film screenings

### Create Cultural Forcing Functions
Set up automatic systems to maintain engagement:
- **Subscriptions**: Literary magazines, art publications, cultural newsletters
- **Commitments**: One cultural documentary per week
- **Social**: Join or start philosophy discussion group
- **Hosting**: Monthly dinner parties with themed cultural discussions
- **Learning**: Online courses in art history, literature, or philosophy

### Document the Journey
Start a cultural learning journal tracking:
- Insights and connections from cultural experiences
- How humanities perspectives influence other interests
- Unexpected discoveries and "aha" moments
- Evolution of aesthetic preferences and intellectual interests
- Connections between different cultural experiences

## Implementation Framework

### Monthly Cultural Commitment (2-3 events)
- 1 visual art experience (museum/gallery)
- 1 literary event (reading, discussion, bookstore event)  
- 1 performance (theater, music, film)

### Seasonal Deep Dives
- **Spring 2025**: Focus on Kent Monkman exhibition + related reading on indigenous perspectives
- **Summer 2025**: Lit Fest + travel prep cultural research for international trips
- **Fall 2025**: Denver Arts Week + Film Festival intensive
- **Winter 2025**: Theater season + philosophy/literature reading season

### Cross-Pollination Approach
Connect each cultural experience to existing interests:
- At Monkman's show: Think about leadership and justice
- At literary events: Explore how great writing tackles psychology studied in personal development
- This prevents cultural consumption from feeling separate from "real" life

### Integration with Existing Plans
- **Date Ideas**: Expand cultural options beyond current list
- **Travel Planning**: Add cultural learning objectives to each trip
- **Personal Development**: Connect humanities insights to growth goals
- **Social Activities**: Incorporate cultural elements into gatherings

## Success Metrics
- Regular engagement rather than intensity (consistency over perfection)
- Ability to draw unexpected connections between disciplines
- Increased appreciation for different perspectives and ways of thinking
- Richer conversations and more diverse interests
- Maintained sense of wonder and intellectual playfulness

## Notes
- Make cultural engagement systematic rather than sporadic
- Connect meaningfully to existing goals and relationships
- Prevent it from feeling like additional burden
- Focus on genuine enrichment of intellectual life
- Allow for organic development of interests rather than forced consumption
