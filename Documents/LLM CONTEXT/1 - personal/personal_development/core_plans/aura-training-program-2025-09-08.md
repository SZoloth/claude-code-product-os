# Aura Training Program (12 Weeks)

Start date: 2025-09-08 — adjust if you begin later.

Purpose: Operational, phase-based system to build metabolic calm, social smoothness, and mission alignment with minimal daily friction.

## How to Use
- Morning: copy the current phase’s Daily Card into your daily plan (`strategic_planning/daily_planning/`), then execute.
- Evening: log outcomes in `journal/entries/YYYY-MM-DD.md` using `journal/templates/daily-reflection.md`.
- Weekly: update your phase’s Weekly Tracker row, plan next week’s gym slots and craft blocks, and schedule social reps.

Related systems
- Cooking: `cooking/Weekly_Meal_Planner_Template.md` (plan protein, dairy, gelatin/broth, fruit sugars)
- Strength: `marathon_training/Strength training.md` (use for compound lift ideas on gym days)
- CRM (social reps log): `crm/interactions/interaction_log.md`
- Daily planning workflow: `strategic_planning/daily_planning/README.md`

## Phase 1 — Metabolic & Physical Base (Weeks 1–4)
Goal: Reduce vascular microspasms by stabilizing energy, hormones, and posture.

Daily
- Nutrition: 3+ meals; include gelatin/broth, dairy, eggs, fruit sugars, red meat or shellfish; salt every meal
- Movement: daily slow walk (shoulders back); 3 gym sessions/week (compound lifts, full ROM, controlled pace)
- Light: 10+ minutes morning sunlight; no screens 1h before bed
- Grooming: nail care; clean shave/trim; moisturizer + sunscreen

Focus drill
- Speak one sentence/day slowly, clearly, into the mirror (smoothness reps)

- [ ] Breakfast anchored; [ ] Lunch; [ ] Dinner; salt each meal

- [ ] Walk, slow with shoulders back (min 10–20 min)

## Phase 2 — Social & Mission Alignment (Weeks 5–8)
Goal: Reduce social jitter; build calm confidence and interesting presence.

Daily (keep all Phase 1 habits)
- Craft/Competence: 30 min hands-on (cooking, carving, writing, etc.)
- Social reps: 3 new micro-interactions/day (barista, stranger, coworker); train eye contact + pauses
- Solitude: 2x/week café/park/event alone; no phone

Focus drill
- End conversations with one curious question instead of over-explaining

Daily Card (copy into today’s plan)
- [ ] Phase 1 habits completed (meals, walk, light, grooming)

## Phase 3 — Aura Consolidation (Weeks 9–12)
Goal: Integrate metabolic calm + social smoothness into a compelling mission.

Daily (keep Phase 1 + 2 habits)
- Mission work: 1 hour focused project (career, art, training — not chasing women)
- Silence practice: once/day hold back a comment you’d normally blurt; feel the agency
- Forgiveness / Release: 5-minute journal to let go of resentments and integrate past

Focus drill
- Weekly “story night”: recount something you did that week and practice making it engaging

Daily Card (copy into today’s plan)
- [ ] Phase 1 + 2 habits completed
- [ ] 1h mission work (project: ______; block scheduled)
- [ ] Silence practice (moment noted)
- [ ] Forgiveness/release journal (5 min)
- [ ] Story night (if today): topic ______
