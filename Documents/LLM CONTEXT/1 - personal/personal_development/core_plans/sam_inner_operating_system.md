# Sam's Inner Operating System (I/O.S. v1)

A modular, evolving field manual. Built for resonance, action, and antifragile growth.

---

## ⚙️ CORE DIRECTIVES

**Non-Negotiables. Prime Commands. These run in the background.**

- Momentum over mastery.
- Sleep is sacred.
- Never produce at the expense of the soul.
- “What do I want them to feel?” → “Do I feel that?” → If no: back to work.
- If it invites a shrug, kill it.
- Iterate in public when possible; refine in private when necessary.
- Don't just consume — **digest**, transform, and reuse.

---

## 🎯 DECISION ENGINES

**Mental models and filters for fast clarity.**

- Regret minimization framework — what would future-me wish I did?
- Satisficing + Spot-checking — is this good enough *for now* to learn?
- Freedom / Leverage / Meaning — evaluate every opportunity through this triad.
- “Would I do this if no one knew I did it?”
- 3x Rule: Do it three times before judging.

---

## 🛠 CREATIVE PROTOCOLS

**Modules for output that leaves a mark.**

### A Field Manual Against Indifference
1. **Intent First:** _What do I want someone to feel?_
2. **Reverse Empathy:** _Do I feel it as a user/viewer?_ If not, back to the board.
3. **No Shrug Work:** Shrug = death. Provoke or move, but never bore.
4. **Let It Echo:** Does it get brought up unprompted? That's signal.
5. **Ship Before You Flinch:** Self-consciousness is the final boss.

### Mise en Place for the Mind
- Set the tone: music, lighting, tools ready.
- Define your creative verb (write, design, map, decide).
- Warm-up: 5 mins of stream-of-consciousness or sketching.

## 🧠 INPUT FILTERS

**Rules for what earns your attention.**

- Read primary sources before commentary.
- If it doesn’t compound — knowledge, skill, or relationship — beware.
- Aesthetic without emotional resonance is ornamental noise.
- If you keep checking it, build a system instead.

## ⚡️ MODES OF OPERATION

**Switch into the right mode for the moment.**

- **Forge Mode** — Deep creation. No feedback, no research.
- **Scout Mode** — Explore, gather inputs. Read, roam, absorb.
- **Architect Mode** — Systematize. Map concepts, organize frameworks.
- **Rogue Mode** — Break your own rules. Act on instinct.

## 🧭 NORTH STARS

**Anchors when drifting.**

- Make things worth remembering.
- Be someone whose ideas travel further than your presence.
- Every project is a brick in the cathedral.
- Play is the testbed of power.

## ❤️ RELATIONSHIP PRINCIPLES

- Name vulnerabilities without controlling responses.
- Practice “Match + 1” — meet and gently stretch emotional openness.
- Let go of managing outcomes. Focus on connection, not control.
- Normalize repair; assume missteps, commit to reattunement.
- Don’t perform understanding. Seek it, sit in ambiguity.
- Self-worth is not up for negotiation.

---

## 🧠 TRAINING PRINCIPLES

- Identity > habit. Train as who you're becoming.
- Build structural strength before chasing speed.
- Long runs on Sundays = weekly non-negotiable.
- Strong legs, strong core, strong mind — the rest follows.
- If you’re tired, differentiate between lazy, under-recovered, or misaligned.

---

## 💰 WEALTH PRINCIPLES

- Time is the true scarce asset; buy it back.
- Play long games with long-term people.
- Avoid lifestyle creep. Increase freedom, not flash.
- Spend where it compounds: health, tools, relationships, ideas.
- Be so good they can’t ignore you — then own the leverage.
