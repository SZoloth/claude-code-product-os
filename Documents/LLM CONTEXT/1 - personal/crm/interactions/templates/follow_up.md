# Follow-up Templates & Guidelines

## Follow-up Strategy Framework

### Timing Guidelines
- **Immediate** (Same day): Thank you messages, urgent action items
- **Short-term** (1-3 days): Promised materials, scheduling next steps
- **Medium-term** (1-2 weeks): Progress updates, value sharing
- **Long-term** (1-3 months): Relationship maintenance, periodic check-ins

### Follow-up Types
1. **Commitment Follow-up**: Delivering on promises made
2. **Value Follow-up**: Sharing relevant insights or connections
3. **Progress Follow-up**: Updating on developments
4. **Relationship Follow-up**: Maintaining connection without specific agenda

## Follow-up Best Practices

### General Guidelines
1. **Keep it brief**: Respect their time with concise messages
2. **Add value**: Always include something useful for them
3. **Be specific**: Reference exact conversations and commitments
4. **Stay organized**: Track follow-ups systematically
5. **Be authentic**: Maintain genuine tone and interest

### Professional Follow-ups
- Always deliver on commitments made
- Reference specific points from your conversation
- Include relevant updates or insights
- Suggest concrete next steps when appropriate

### Personal Follow-ups
- Share genuine life updates
- Ask about their interests and activities
- Suggest specific plans or activities
- Remember important dates and events

### Job Search Follow-ups
- Thank everyone who provides time or advice
- Update on job search progress when relevant
- Share outcomes of introductions or referrals
- Maintain relationships regardless of immediate opportunities

### When Not to Follow Up
- After three unreturned attempts
- When they've explicitly requested no follow-up
- During busy periods they've mentioned
- When the relationship has naturally concluded

### Follow-up Frequency
- **Active opportunities**: Weekly to bi-weekly
- **Professional relationships**: Monthly to quarterly
- **Personal relationships**: As natural based on closeness
- **Dormant relationships**: Bi-annually or annually
