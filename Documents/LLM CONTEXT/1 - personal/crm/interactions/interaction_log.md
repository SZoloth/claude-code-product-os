## Interaction Types

### Professional
- **Meetings**: Formal business meetings
- **Coffee Chats**: Informal professional conversations
- **Phone Calls**: Business-related calls
- **Email Exchanges**: Significant email conversations
- **Conference/Events**: Professional networking events
- **Mentoring**: Sessions with mentors or mentees

### Personal
- **Social Activities**: Dinners, parties, gatherings
- **Phone/Video Calls**: Personal catch-up conversations
- **Activities**: Shared hobbies, sports, adventures
- **Family Time**: Family gatherings and conversations
- **Community Events**: Local community involvement

### Job Search
- **Networking Meetings**: Job search focused conversations
- **Informational Interviews**: Learning about companies/roles
- **Job Interviews**: Formal interview processes
- **JSC Meetings**: Job Search Council sessions
- **Recruiter Calls**: Conversations with recruiters
- **Follow-ups**: Post-interview or post-connection follow-ups
