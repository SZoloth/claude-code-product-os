# Opportunity Scorecard Template

Purpose: Evaluate interview opportunities and offers consistently. Weight by what matters most. Use as a live document during loops and when negotiating.

## Instructions
- Copy this file into the specific role folder as `opportunity-scorecard.md`.
- Score each criterion 1–5 (1 = poor, 5 = excellent). Multiply by weight. Sum to 100.
- Note any red flags. Red flags can override score.

## Notes & Evidence
- Role Fit: KPIs you’ll own; linkage to business outcomes; clarity of success definition.
- Manager Fit: communication style, expectations, track record, sponsorship.
- Team & Culture: decision cadence, psychological safety, cross‑functional norms.
- Scope & Influence: access to data/tools, decision rights, board/executive exposure.
- Growth & Learning: mentorship, IC/manager path, domain depth you’ll build.
- Compensation: structure, vesting, refreshes, benefits; negotiation flexibility.
- Stability & Runway: cash runway, revenue quality, market position, risk factors.
- Logistics: office policy, travel expectations, time zone fit.

## Red Flags (Override if severe)
- Undefined success metrics or constantly shifting scope
- High attrition on the team or manager turnover
- Misalignment between stated goals and incentives/resources
- Off‑market compensation with little flexibility
- Ethical concerns or cultural mismatches

## Decision Rules (Guidance)
- Strong Fit: ≥ 80 with no critical red flags → proceed confidently
- On the Fence: 65–79 or unresolved red flags → clarify before proceeding
- Mismatch: < 65 or critical red flags → deprioritize
