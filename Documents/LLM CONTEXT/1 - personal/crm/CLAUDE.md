# CLAUDE.md - Customer Relationship Management (CRM)

## Folder Purpose

This folder contains comprehensive relationship management systems for tracking contacts, interactions, and relationship-building activities across personal and professional contexts.

## Key Guidelines

- **Relationship-Centric**: Focus on maintaining and strengthening meaningful connections
- **Systematic Tracking**: Maintain organized records of interactions and follow-ups
- **Privacy Conscious**: Handle personal information responsibly and securely
- **Action-Oriented**: Support proactive relationship management and networking

## Content Organization

### Contact Management
- **contacts/**: Directory containing categorized contact information
  - **contacts_master.md**: Complete contact database
  - **job_search.md**: Career-related professional contacts
  - **personal.md**: Personal relationships and friendships
  - **professional.md**: Business and professional network

### Interaction Management  
- **interactions/**: Communication tracking and templates
  - **interaction_log.md**: Record of meaningful interactions
  - **templates/**: Email templates and outreach frameworks
    - **email_outreach.md**: Professional outreach templates
    - **follow_up.md**: Follow-up communication templates
    - **meeting_notes.md**: Meeting documentation template

### Special Tracking
- **Carter_Gift_Ideas_Tracker.md**: Personal gift planning for important relationships
- **my-linkedin-connections.csv**: LinkedIn network data

## Working with this Content

When working with files in this folder:

1. **Maintain Privacy**: Protect personal information and contact details appropriately
2. **Regular Updates**: Keep interaction logs and contact information current
3. **Relationship Insights**: Track relationship dynamics and important personal details
4. **Follow-up Systems**: Maintain systematic approach to relationship nurturing
