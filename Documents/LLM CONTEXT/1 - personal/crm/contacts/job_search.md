### Relationship Development Stages
1. **Initial Contact**: First introduction or outreach
2. **Building Rapport**: Getting to know each other, sharing insights
3. **Value Exchange**: Providing mutual value, industry insights
4. **Opportunity Discussion**: Exploring potential roles and fit
5. **Active Candidate**: In interview process or under consideration
6. **Long-term Relationship**: Ongoing professional relationship regardless of immediate opportunities

### Communication Templates

- Thank you notes
- Value-sharing content

### Tracking Metrics
- Response rates to outreach
- Conversion from contact to conversation
- Referral success rates
- Time from first contact to opportunity
- Relationship progression through stages
