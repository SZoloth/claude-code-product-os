## Networking Strategy

### Relationship Maintenance
- **Strong Relationships**: Contact monthly, provide value regularly
- **Medium Relationships**: Contact quarterly, share relevant content
- **Weak Relationships**: Contact bi-annually, maintain awareness

### Value Provision
- Share relevant articles and insights
- Make introductions between contacts
- Offer assistance with projects/challenges
- Provide feedback and advice when requested

### Professional Development
- Seek mentorship from senior professionals
- Build peer relationships for knowledge sharing
- Maintain connections with rising professionals
- Cultivate relationships with industry thought leaders
