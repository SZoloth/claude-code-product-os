# 20‑Miler Dress Rehearsal — Checklist (2025-09-26)

Purpose (added 2025-09-23): Single-page prep for the dress rehearsal long run. Time cap 3:00. Target: miles 6–16 @ 8:47 MP.

## Go/No‑Go — Thu 2025-09-25, 7:00 pm
- RHR ≤ +5–7 bpm vs baseline; HRV not suppressed
- Sleep ≥ 6.5 h Wed→Thu; legs feel reasonably fresh
- If any flag: move the 20‑miler to Sat 2025-09-27 and make Fri 4–5 mi easy + strides

## Route & Logistics
- Course: low‑stoplight route or multi‑loop w/ access to fluids; aim ~20.0 mi
- Time cap 3:00 — stop at 18–19 if approaching cap
- Weather: check temp/wind; adjust early pace by 2–4% if >60°F
- Drop bottles/handheld: fluids every ~2–3 mi (electrolytes + water)

## Breakfast (2.5–3 h pre‑run)
- Carbs 100–150 g; moderate protein; low fat/fiber
- Coffee if typical; finish sipping 60–90 min pre‑run

## During Run (30–60 g carbs/hour)
- Gel schedule: ~35, 70, 100, 130 min (adjust as needed)
- Fluids: alternate water/electrolyte; small sips at each aid point
- Practice exactly race‑day gel/flavor/brand; same belt/handheld

## Pacing Plan
- Miles 0–5: easy settling (≈9:30–9:45)
- Miles 6–16: marathon pace 8:47/mi
- Miles 17–20: back to easy; finish relaxed

## Gear
- Shoes: race or race‑adjacent pair; socks you’ll race in
- Kit: race‑day shorts/top; BodyGlide; watch; charger; ID
- Safety: phone, $10 cash/card, RoadID

## Post‑Run Recovery (within 30–45 min)
- 20–30 g protein + 60–90 g carbs; hydrate w/ electrolytes
- Light stretch; optional 5–10 min cold shower on calves
- Easy walk later in day; early bedtime
