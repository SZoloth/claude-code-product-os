## Training Philosophy

### Systematic Approach
- **Progressive overload**: Gradual increase in training volume and intensity
- **Consistency over intensity**: Prioritizing regular training over sporadic high-intensity efforts
- **Recovery integration**: Building rest and recovery into training cycles

### Performance Integration
- **Holistic preparation**: Training encompasses running, strength, nutrition, and mental preparation
- **Data-driven decisions**: Using training log data to adjust plans and track progress
- **Long-term development**: Building sustainable running practices beyond single race goals

## How to Use This System

### Daily Training
1. **Check master_mcm_training_plan.md** for scheduled workout
2. **Execute training** according to plan guidelines
3. **Log results** in mcm_training_log.md with notes on performance and feelings
4. **Adjust if needed** based on fatigue, injury, or life circumstances

### Weekly/Monthly Review
- **Analyze training log** for patterns and progress trends
- **Compare actual vs. planned** training volume and intensity
- **Update plans** based on performance and upcoming schedule

### Race Preparation
- **Reference training guides** for race strategy and preparation
- **Review nutrition plans** (integrated with Cooking Recipes Meal Planning)
- **Mental preparation** using training log confidence building
