# Knee & Shin Recovery Routine

## Daily Routine (15-20 minutes)
*Perform every day until symptoms resolve, then 3x/week for prevention*

### Phase 1: Mobility & Stretching (8-10 minutes)

#### IT Band & Hip Mobility
1. **Standing IT Band Stretch**
   - Stand arm's length from wall
   - Cross affected leg behind other leg
   - Lean away from wall, feel stretch along outside of leg
   - Hold: 30 seconds x 2 reps each side

2. **Figure-4 Hip Flexor Stretch**
   - Kneeling position, affected leg forward
   - Push hips forward, keep back straight
   - Feel stretch in front of back leg's hip
   - Hold: 30 seconds each leg

3. **Modified Pigeon Pose**
   - Seated or lying down variation
   - Ankle of affected leg on opposite knee
   - Gently pull knee toward chest
   - Hold: 45 seconds each side

### Phase 2: Targeted Strengthening (7-10 minutes)

#### Hip & Glute Activation
4. **Clamshells with Resistance Band**
   - Lie on side, knees bent 90 degrees
   - Band around knees
   - Lift top knee while keeping feet together
   - Reps: 15 x 2 sets each side

5. **Side-lying Leg Lifts**
   - Lie on side, legs straight
   - Lift top leg straight up
   - Lower with control
   - Reps: 12 x 2 sets each side

6. **Single-leg Glute Bridges**
   - Lie on back, one foot on ground
   - Lift opposite leg straight
   - Bridge up with grounded leg
   - Reps: 10 x 2 sets each leg

### Phase 3: Shin & Ankle Care (3-5 minutes)

#### Anterior Tibialis Relief
7. **Toe Walks**
   - Walk on toes for 20 steps forward
   - Walk on heels for 20 steps backward
   - Focus on controlled movement

8. **Calf Stretches Against Wall**
   - Hands on wall, step back with affected leg
   - Keep heel down, lean forward
   - Hold: 30 seconds x 2 each leg

9. **Ankle Circles**
   - Seated or lying down
   - Slowly circle each ankle
   - 10 circles each direction, each foot

## When to Progress

### Continue Daily Until:
- [ ] No pain going up/down stairs
- [ ] No tenderness when pressing outside of knee
- [ ] Hip flexors feel loose and mobile

### Then Transition to:
- **Maintenance**: 3x per week
- **Add strengthening**: Single-leg stands, lateral band walks
- **Return to normal training**: Gradually increase intensity

## Notes
- **Consistency over intensity** - better to do this routine daily at moderate intensity
- **Ice after longer runs** if inflammation present
- **Stop if pain increases** during any exercise
- **Most improvement seen**: 7-14 days with daily practice
