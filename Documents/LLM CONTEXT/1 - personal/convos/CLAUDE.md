# CLAUDE.md - Conversations

## Folder Purpose

This folder contains documentation of important conversations, discussions, and meeting notes that provide context for personal and professional relationships.

## Key Guidelines

- **Relationship Context**: All content captures important interactions that inform relationship management
- **Historical Record**: Maintain accurate records of meaningful conversations and their outcomes
- **Follow-up Tracking**: Support identification of action items and commitments from conversations
- **Privacy Conscious**: Respect confidentiality while maintaining useful reference information

## Content Organization

- **README.md**: Overview of conversation documentation system
- Individual conversation files (e.g., "carter cook call.md"): Specific conversation records with key takeaways

## Working with this Content

When working with files in this folder:

1. **Accuracy**: Ensure conversation summaries accurately reflect what was discussed
2. **Action Items**: Clearly identify and track follow-up commitments
3. **Context Preservation**: Capture enough detail to provide useful future reference
4. **Relationship Insights**: Note important personal or professional insights gained

## Agent Recommendations

- Use **relationship-connection-coach** agent for analyzing conversation patterns and relationship dynamics
- Use **crm** folder integration for connecting conversations to ongoing relationship management
- Use **job-search-strategist** agent when conversations relate to career opportunities or professional networking
